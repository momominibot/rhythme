Your health notes, in your hands

Choose a real record to keep. Nothing here infers a diagnosis or activates a cloud account.

Bring the whole picture

Keep medicine, supplement and treatment information for a conversation with your care team. A record is what you enter, not an automated prescription.

Keep measurements distinct

Temperature, urine LH results, symptoms and lab reports have different meanings and limitations. Record their dates, methods and original units separately.

Your choices remain yours
