Small choices, thoughtfully made

Supplement needs are personal. This page does not create a dose or deficiency from a profile.

Preparing for pregnancy

Standard guidance commonly recommends 400 micrograms of folic acid daily when pregnancy is possible or planned. Some people need a prescribed dose. Check with your clinician rather than combining products blindly.

Bring every product to the conversation

List prescribed medicines, over-the-counter products, herbs and supplements with their exact labels. Ask your care team about interactions and suitability. Do not stop prescribed treatment on your own.

Tests are context, not an automatic plan

Save dated lab results with their original units and reference information. Missing results do not mean deficiency, and a tracker cannot choose a personal replacement dose.

Traditional does not mean safe for everyone

Medicinal herbs and mixed formulas can interact with medicines and vary in ingredients. Pregnancy, breastfeeding and childhood call for qualified review of the exact product.
