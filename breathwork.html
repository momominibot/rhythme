Resonance Breathing

Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s. Shown to reduce cortisol and improve HRV. Best before bed or during high-stress moments.

Box Breathing

Equal inhale, hold, exhale, hold. Used by athletes and clinicians to quickly down-regulate the nervous system.

Coherent Breathing

5.5 seconds in, 5.5 seconds out. Targets cardiac resonance frequency, maximizing heart rate variability.

Why this helps ovaries

Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which can disrupt GnRH pulsatility and ovarian function. Regular breathwork is a free, evidence-based tool to support hormonal resilience.
