Community Guidelines

We keep Circle safe and supportive
💛
Be kind and supportive. Every person's journey is different. No judgment, no unsolicited advice unless asked.
🔒
Respect privacy. Don't share personal information — yours or others'. Anonymous posting is available for sensitive topics.
🚫
No medical diagnosis. Community shares are experiences, not medical advice. Always consult a healthcare provider for personal concerns.
📢
No spam or self-promotion. Keep posts relevant to women's health journeys. Report inappropriate content — we moderate actively.
🌈
Inclusive space. We welcome all women and people with cycles, regardless of background, identity, or experience.
💛 Community, not clinical care. Circle is a peer support space. Posts reflect individual experiences and opinions, not medical advice. Evidence markers (i) link to general health information. For personal medical concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
