⚠️ Evidence Corrections — What the Research Actually Shows
1. Dong Quai alone does NOT reduce hot flashes. The only rigorous RCT (n=71) was definitively negative i. Dong quai works only in multi-herb formulas like Danggui Buxue Tang i.
2. Flaxseed does NOT reduce hot flashes better than placebo. Phase III RCT (n=188) was null i. Flaxseed is still nutritious for fiber and ALA.
3. Pueraria mirifica ≠ Pueraria lobata (Chinese 葛根). Hot flash evidence is for the Thai species P. mirifica, which contains potent phytoestrogens (miroestrol). Chinese kudzu has lower phytoestrogen content i.
4. Soy isoflavone extract ≠ black bean. Black bean contains isoflavones at lower concentrations than soybean. Do not attribute soy study results (20.6% hot flash reduction) to black bean i.
5. 8 herbs have NO human clinical data for women's health: astragalus, goji, ligustrum, mulberry, motherwort, salvia, ophiopogon, and black bean. All evidence is preclinical (animal/in vitro) only.
6. Cochrane rates TCM formulas overall as very low quality (60 trials, n=6,059). No single formula can be broadly recommended i.

当归补血汤 Danggui Buxue Tang i
黄芪30g : 当归6g (5:1 golden ratio)
Dosage6g/day (as prepared formula), or 黄芪30g+当归6g decoction
FrequencyDaily 1 dose, 2-3 consecutive cycles; post-period only
Best PhasePost-period (blood tonification golden window); menopause
Preparation600ml water → 300ml, split into 2 warm servings; add 5 red dates
Traditional use: From 李东垣《内外伤辨惑论》. "Qi-blood double tonification" for post-period weakness, postpartum recovery, and menopausal hot flashes.
Evidence: ✅ Best-evidenced TCM formula for hot flashes. Dose-escalation RCT (n=60): 6g/day → 34.9-37.4% hot flash reduction (P<0.01), night sweats -39.6%; 3g/day → 14.5-21.2%; 1.5g/day not significant i. Separate RCT (n=103): significant reduction in mild hot flashes. No estrogenic effects.
⚠️ Cautions: Stop during period. Not for acute infection or fever. Best for mild-to-moderate hot flashes; severe may not respond. Consult a TCM practitioner.

甘麦大枣汤 Gan Mai Da Zao Tang i
甘草6g + 淮小麦30g + 大枣3枚
Dosage甘草6-10g (honey-fried preferred) + 淮小麦15-30g + 大枣3-10枚
FrequencyDaily 1 dose; can be daily tea; mild cases 2x/week
Best PhasePerimenopause "脏躁" (emotional lability); pre-period low mood; no cycle restriction
Preparation800ml → 400ml, drink soup + eat dates; or congee; use 淮小麦 NOT 浮小麦
Traditional use: From《金匮要略》. For "脏躁" — emotional lability, crying, yawning, insomnia. All food-grade ingredients (wheat, licorice, jujube).
Evidence: 🟡 Meta-analyses (35 RCTs pooled): GMDZ significantly alleviated depression/anxiety, improved sleep; combination with antidepressants > alone i. Overall evidence quality LOW (heterogeneity, unclear allocation, lack of blinding). Network pharmacology: estrogen receptor, serotonergic, inflammatory pathways.
⚠️ Cautions: Very safe (all food-grade). Use 淮小麦 (sinks in water, heart-calming) NOT 浮小麦 (stops sweating). Long-term high-dose licorice can cause water retention/hypokalemia — monitor if hypertensive.

逍遥丸 Xiao Yao Wan i
柴胡, 当归, 白芍, 白术, 茯苓, 炙甘草, 薄荷, 生姜
Dosage6-9g water pills 1-2x/day; large honey pill 1 pill 2x/day
FrequencyStart 1 week pre-period; 2-4 weeks = 1 course; stop when symptoms improve
Best PhasePre-period (7 days before) — liver-depression spleen-deficiency PMS
PreparationOTC patent medicine; take 1 hour after meals with warm water
Traditional use: "Soothe liver, strengthen spleen, nourish blood, regulate menstruation." Go-to OTC formula for PMS mood, irritability, breast tenderness, and menstrual irregularity.
Evidence: ✅ Jiawei Xiaoyao pill (modified with 牡丹皮+栀子): multi-center RCT n=144, 12g/day × 3 cycles → significant PMS symptom improvement vs placebo i. ⚠️ Evidence is for Jiawei (modified) version, not classic Xiao Yao Wan. Meta-analysis for perimenopause: superior to HRT but Chinese, unblinded, high bias.
⚠️ Cautions: Evidence specifically for Jiawei version. Not for daily maintenance — requires pattern differentiation. Avoid cold/raw foods during use. Consult a TCM practitioner.

28-Day Cycle Tea Protocol
Four-phase traditional tea template (no RCTs of this specific protocol)
🩸 Menstrual (Days 1-5) — Warm uterus, dispel cold干姜3g + 红枣3颗 + 红糖. Daily 1 cup, days 1-3. Heavy menses: only ginger-date tea (no dong quai/motherwort/safflower).
🌱 Follicular (Days 6-12) — Tonify qi/blood, nourish yin黄芪6g + 当归2g + 党参3g + 红枣3颗 + 麦冬3g + 玉竹3g. Daily 1 cup × 7 days from period end ("进补黄金期").
✨ Ovulation (Days 13-16) — Nourish ovaries, warm kidney黑豆10g + 桑葚5-8g + 桂圆3颗 + 枸杞6-10粒 + 红枣2-3颗. Daily 1 cup × 7 days around ovulation.
🌙 Luteal (Days 17-28) — Soothe liver, move qi玫瑰花5-6朵 + 陈皮2-3g + 桑葚5g + 桂圆2颗 + 红枣3颗. Daily 1 cup, 7 days pre-period. Rose added last, don't boil.
Traditional use: Widely circulated on Chinese platforms (小红书/抖音). Phase logic aligns with TCM cycle theory: warm during period, tonify after, nourish at ovulation, soothe pre-period.
Evidence: 🟡 No RCTs of this specific protocol. Individual ingredients have varying evidence (see individual herb cards). The phase-aligned framework is traditional, not clinically validated. Frame as wellness practice, not clinical treatment.
⚠️ Cautions: Blood-activating herbs (益母草, 红花, 丹参) are pregnancy contraindicated and cycle-gated. Heavy menses: avoid dong quai/motherwort/safflower during period. Consult a TCM practitioner for personalized formula.
block Foods to Limit▼
These may worsen cycle symptoms
🍟
Processed & Fried Foods
Why: High in trans fats and sodium, which can increase inflammation and water retention — worsening bloating and breast tenderness. They also disrupt blood sugar balance, amplifying mood swings and cravings.
☕
Excessive Caffeine i
Why: More than 200-300mg/day (2-3 cups) can worsen breast tenderness, anxiety, and sleep disruption — especially in the luteal phase. Caffeine also affects cortisol levels and can amplify stress responses i.
🍷
Alcohol i
Why: Disrupts sleep quality (especially REM sleep), affects liver hormone metabolism, and can worsen PMS mood symptoms. It may also increase hot flash frequency in perimenopause i. Limit to occasional use.
🍬
High-Sugar Foods
Why: Rapid blood sugar spikes and crashes worsen energy fluctuations, irritability, and cravings — particularly in the luteal phase. Excess sugar also promotes inflammation and can disrupt gut microbiome balance, affecting hormone metabolism.
