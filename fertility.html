Your fertile window

Pregnancy can start from sex in the six days that end on the day of ovulation: the five days before it and the day itself. The chance is highest in the last two or three of those days.

Wilcox, Weinberg and Baird, New England Journal of Medicine, 1995, 221 women. PubMed

Your own estimate

Reading your calendar…

Why a calendar can only estimate

Ovulation often comes about two weeks before the next period, but it moves. In a study of 221 women, the textbook days 10 to 17 held the whole fertile window for only about 3 in 10 women, and timing could be hard to predict even when cycles were usually regular.

Wilcox, Dunson and Baird, BMJ, 2000. PubMed

Signs you can notice

water_drop
Cervical mucus
In the days before ovulation it often turns clear, wet and stretchy, like raw egg white. It is a sign that the window may be open, not a guarantee.
science
Urine LH tests
A positive strip shows a hormone surge that usually comes a day or so before ovulation. Follow the kit’s instructions; a positive test does not prove that ovulation happened.
device_thermostat
Waking temperature
It rises a little after ovulation, so it tells you afterwards rather than ahead. Late nights, shifts, illness and alcohol can all move it.

What helps, and when to ask

You do not have to hit exact days. NICE advises that sex every 2 to 3 days through the cycle gives a good chance of pregnancy, and many people find that easier than timing.

NICE guideline NG257, initial advice. NICE

Ask a doctor after 12 months of trying if you are under 35, or after 6 months from 35. Ask sooner if your periods are irregular or absent, very painful, you have a known condition, or you simply want to talk it through. A polyclinic or GP can start tests and refer you to KKH or NUH.

ASRM committee opinion, 2021. ASRM

Get care now
One-sided tummy pain, shoulder-tip pain, fainting or heavy bleeding when your period is late or you could be pregnant needs urgent assessment, even with a negative test. Call 995 now if the pain is severe, you feel faint or weak, or the bleeding is heavy. Otherwise go with someone to KKH’s 24-hour Urgent O&G Centre or an A&E.
