A gentle, source-linked guide

Fertility signals, with room for uncertainty

Source-checked 30 September 2026. Personal advice belongs in a conversation with your clinician.

A window, not a guaranteed day

The fertile window is generally described as the days leading up to and including ovulation. Calendar dates vary between cycles; a predicted date does not prove egg release.

Your signals are observations

Cervical fluid, waking temperature and home urine tests can offer useful context. Record the test brand, date, units or result and anything that might affect it. A single sign cannot establish ovarian reserve, an ovarian age or pregnancy.

What Ovuluna does

The app keeps observations and lab results that you explicitly enter. It has no validated 96% ovulation detector, connected wearable ring or automatic fertility diagnosis. The camera pulse check is experimental and does not replace fertility care.
