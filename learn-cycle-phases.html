The pattern, gently explained

The follicular phase begins with the first day of bleeding. Developing follicles produce hormones as the body prepares for possible ovulation. The luteal phase follows ovulation. This is a helpful map, not a prediction of what day your body must follow.

A ring is an illustration

Ovulation does not always occur on day 14. The glowing 28-day ring in Ovuluna is a teaching example. Your dated bleeding and symptom records stay separate from it.

How you feel matters

A phase name cannot tell us your mood, energy or ideal workout. Notice your own symptoms and choose movement that feels manageable. Changes that disrupt school, work or everyday life deserve support.
