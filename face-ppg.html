Camera pulse readings are sensitive to light, movement and skin tone, and beat-to-beat variability from a 30-second face video is noisy. Treat the calm index as a wellness cue, never as a stress diagnosis. Finger-on-camera readings (the fingerprint button) are usually steadier.

scienceWhat the open-source field says

Face pulse (remote photoplethysmography, rPPG) reads tiny colour changes in the skin. This page uses the simplest robust method, the green channel of a forehead patch, plus the app’s existing peak detector. The best-validated open-source toolkit for face pulse research is rPPG-Toolbox (NeurIPS 2023, about 1.2k stars, Python, with classic POS/CHROM and trained DeepPhys/TS-CAN models); it is the reference to validate against before any stress claim. A 2024 study found uncontrolled webcam conditions can severely degrade accuracy, so readings here are labelled experimental.
