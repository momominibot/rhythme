What each hormone means

EstrogenThickens the lining and builds fertile mucus as it rises through the follicular phase.
ProgesteroneClimbs after ovulation to support the luteal phase; a drop triggers your period.
LH · Luteinizing HormoneIts mid-cycle surge is the direct trigger that releases the egg within ~24–36h.
FSH · Follicle StimulatingRecruits and matures the follicle early in the cycle before estrogen takes over.
