What Your Numbers Mean

monitor_heart
HRV (Heart Rate Variability)
The variation between consecutive heartbeats. Higher HRV means your nervous system is flexible and resilient. Women's HRV fluctuates across the cycle, often peaking in the follicular phase i.
sentiment_stressed
Stress Score
Derived from HRV patterns and pulse wave analysis. 0–30 = low, 31–60 = moderate, 61+ = high. Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which can affect cycle regularity i.
cardiology
Vascular Age
Estimated from pulse wave velocity and arterial stiffness. A vascular age younger than your chronological age suggests good cardiovascular health. Estrogen supports vascular function i.
pulmonology
Respiratory Rate
Breaths per minute at rest. Normal range is 12–20. Slow, deep breathing (6 breaths/min) can increase HRV and reduce stress i.
smartphone
How PPG Works
Your phone's camera detects subtle color changes in your fingertip caused by blood flow. This optical signal is analyzed to extract heart rate, HRV, and vascular indicators i.
