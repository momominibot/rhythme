Supported inputs and entry limits

These software limits catch entry mistakes; they are not normal lab ranges or a claim that the model is validated across every value. Values outside these limits remain suitable for your own lab records, but cannot be calculated here.

  • Age at blood draw: 20–100 years.
  • Albumin: 20–60 g/L; creatinine: 20–400 µmol/L.
  • Serum glucose: 2–30 mmol/L; CRP: greater than 0 and up to 50 mg/dL.
  • Lymphocytes: 5–70%; MCV: 60–120 fL.
  • RDW-CV: 10–25% (not RDW-SD in fL).
  • ALP: 20–500 U/L; WBC: 2–30 × 10³/µL.

The same limits apply after converting your selected units. A CRP value of zero cannot be used because the formula takes its natural logarithm. Outputs outside 0–120 years are not displayed; this is a software sanity boundary, not a validated clinical interval.

What the formula can tell you

Clinical PhenoAge combines nine blood markers and chronological age. The original research developed the model in US adults aged 20 and older. This page uses the rounded coefficients printed in the 2018 supplement, so values may differ slightly from implementations with more decimal places.

This is distinct from the DNA methylation model called DNAm PhenoAge. A number here cannot establish ovarian reserve, egg quality, fertility, your individual life expectancy or the effect of a lifestyle change.
