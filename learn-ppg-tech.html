A pulse, seen through light

How your camera notices a fingertip pulse, and why a clear signal still needs careful interpretation.

  3. 3

Background: Bolkhovsky et al., 2012.

Two numbers, different questions

Pulse rate

How often the detected pulse repeats, expressed as beats per minute.

PRV · RMSSD

How much neighboring pulse intervals differ, expressed in milliseconds.

Camera pulse-rate variability (PRV) uses optical pulses. ECG heart-rate variability (HRV) uses electrical heartbeat timing. Agreement depends on the method and recording conditions.

See a worked RMSSD example

Example only: three pulse intervals are 1,000, 1,020 and 980 ms. The successive changes are +20 and −40 ms.

Square each change, take their mean, then its square root: √((20² + (−40)²) / 2) ≈ 31.6 ms.

This explains the arithmetic. Accurate peak timing and suitable signal quality are separate requirements.

What the studies actually tested

Evidence has a specific scope

A 2017 study compared smartphone PPG with ECG in 29 healthy people during five-minute resting recordings. A separate 2022 study evaluated a fingertip-camera heart-rate algorithm in 95 participants.

These studies tested their own devices and software. They do not validate Ovuluna’s camera engine, establish its HRV error, or support an ovulation-accuracy claim.

Ovuluna · Experimental camera check

Try a quiet observation

The current check collects a 60-second recording after placement. Follow its on-screen instructions, keep your hand still and stop if the flash feels hot. If finger contact is lost, the recording is discarded and restarted.

A result passing the software checks is still an experimental estimate. It does not confirm ovulation, diagnose stress or change a fasting plan.
