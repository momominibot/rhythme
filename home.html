favorite You are not alone 💚

1 in 7 women experience postpartum depression. If you’re feeling overwhelmed, sad, or disconnected, please reach out. In Singapore: call SOS 1-767 (24/7) or text 741741. Talk to your doctor or midwife at your 6-week checkup. i

⚠️ When to call your doctor:

Heavy bleeding (soaking a pad in 1 hour), fever >38°C, severe headache, chest pain, difficulty breathing, suicidal thoughts, or incision redness/swelling.

Log hot flashes, night sweats, and mood daily to see patterns. Perimenopause can last 4-8 years before your final period. Tracking helps you and your doctor decide if HRT or other support is right for you. i

🦴 Bone health matters more now

After menopause, bone density drops rapidly. Aim for 1000-1200mg calcium and 800-1000 IU vitamin D daily, plus weight-bearing exercise 3-4x/week. Ask your doctor about a bone density scan after age 65 (or earlier if at risk). i
