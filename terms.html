6 Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement reasonable security measures to protect it:

  • Local storage: Your data is stored on your device using browser localStorage, which is isolated to your browser and device

Device security is your responsibility: Since data is stored locally on your device, please ensure your device is protected with a passcode, biometric lock, or other security measures. We are not responsible for data accessed from an unlocked or lost device.

Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent to collection, use, or disclosure of your data at any time
  • Right to Data Portability: You may export your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact the privacy contact named in the full privacy policy (being written now). We will respond to your request within 30 days.
