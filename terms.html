4 How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for these purposes:

  • To run the App on your phone: cycle tracking, logging, reminders, patterns and the summaries you ask for
  • To back up and restore your diary, if you turn on cloud backup
  • Luna's AI help, currently paused (see section 3)

We will not use your personal data for any other purpose without asking you first.

5 Consent & Withdrawal

Keeping your diary on your phone is what the App is, so it is the one required choice. Every other use is a separate choice: cloud backup, Help improve logging, plaza groups and plaza friends (Luna's AI help is paused). New choices start off, choices you made before are shown as you left them, and every choice is recorded with the notice version you read.

You can withdraw at any time, as easily as you agreed:

  • Turn any choice off under Your choices in You, or in Luna's settings
  • Turn cloud backup off, or delete your account and its cloud copy, from Your account
  • Leave a plaza group, or turn Plaza friends off
  • Or write to us at the address in section 17

Turning something off stops that use from then on. Some features cannot work without the data they need.

6 Data Storage & Security

  • On your phone: your diary is saved in this browser's storage. It is not encrypted by the App, so anyone who can open this browser on your phone may be able to read it. Please lock your phone with a passcode or biometrics.
  • Cloud backup (optional): kept in a database in Singapore. Each account can read only its own rows. Our server functions and a small number of authorised staff can reach the database to run the service, and every staff action is logged.
  • In transit: everything between the App and our servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • What staff see: the staff page shows counts and group averages, never anyone's diary. When someone reports a plaza card or note, staff see the two plaza cards (the names and looks people chose) and the reported phrase, to decide the report. Staff records name the staff member and what they did.

If a data breach happens, we will contain it and assess it as soon as practicable, aiming to finish within 30 days. If it is notifiable under the PDPA, we will tell the Personal Data Protection Commission as soon as practicable and within 3 calendar days of deciding so, and tell the people affected where the law requires it, with steps they can take.

Write to us at the address in section 17. We reply to access requests as soon as reasonably possible, make corrections as soon as practicable, and if we need longer than 30 days we tell you in writing within that time when we will reply. While we work on a request, we keep the data it asks about.
