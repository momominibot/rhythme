Before your room opens

What Rhythme keeps, where it goes, and the choices that stay yours.

  • edit_note
    What you logPeriods, symptoms, pain, mood, sleep, meals and food photos, weight, calm minutes, and your getting-to-know-you answers, which can include pregnancy, hormones, medicines and health history.
  • smartphone
    Kept on this phone unless you choose backupYour diary stays in this browser, so clearing the browser or losing the phone can lose it. You can export a copy any time from You. Cloud backup is a separate choice below: a copy kept in your account in a secure database hosted in Singapore, which other people cannot read and Rhythme’s server handles only to back up, restore and delete your diary.
  • auto_awesome
    Luna’s AI help is your choiceReading a note, naming the food in a photo and writing a journal reading are three separate choices. Each stays off until you turn it on. Your name is never added, and Rhythme keeps none of it.
  • lock
    What we will not doWe do not sell your data or use it for ads, and Rhythme does not keep what Luna reads for you.
  • dns
    Ordinary service recordsOur web host keeps standard connection logs and fonts load from Google. Using a Rhythme Plus code records that use with a scrambled network address, to stop misuse. There is no advertising, and no analytics unless you turn on “Help improve logging” below; then only group counts and averages across at least 20 adults are produced.
  • tune
    You stay in controlEvery choice below can be turned off any time from You, as easily as it was turned on, and each change is kept on record with the notice version you read. Export your diary, or delete everything on this phone, any time from You.
  • medical_information
    A friend, not a doctorPredictions and Luna can be wrong. Rhythme does not diagnose, treat, or work as contraception. In an emergency in Singapore, call 995.
  • cake
    For adultsRhythme is for people 18 and over. We keep only your confirmation of that, never your date of birth.
