Coherent Breathing
Follicular · Energy & Focus
Slow, rhythmic breathing at 6 breaths per minute. This maximizes heart rate variability (HRV), balances your autonomic nervous system, and boosts the energy and clarity your follicular phase naturally supports i. HRV is a key marker of stress resilience and overall wellness in women i.

Anytime · Stress Relief

Diaphragmatic Breathing
Menses · Pain Relief
Deep belly breathing directly targets cramp physiology. It increases intra-abdominal pressure, improves uterine blood flow, reduces prostaglandin secretion, and decreases uterine smooth muscle contraction intensity. Studies show a significant decrease in menstrual symptoms vs control (p=0.0008) i.
4s Inhale
6s Exhale
5m Per session
lightbulb

How: Lie on your back, knees bent, one hand on your chest and the other on your abdomen. Inhale slowly through your nose, letting your abdomen rise (hand on belly moves out, chest stays still). Exhale gently through pursed lips, contracting your abdomen. Keep breaths slow and deep.

Benson Relaxation Technique
Daily · Long-term Relief
Developed by Dr. Herbert Benson, this technique induces the "relaxation response" through regulated breathing and a focus word. It effectively reduces menstrual pain intensity and anxiety in adolescent girls. Mechanisms include decreased sympathetic activity, stimulated endogenous endorphin release, and reduced stress hormones i.
10-20m Per session
1-2x Daily
spa Focus word
lightbulb

How: Sit quietly in a comfortable position. Close your eyes. Relax your muscles, starting from your feet and progressing upward. Breathe naturally through your nose. As you breathe out, silently repeat a focus word or phrase (e.g., "calm," "one," "peace," or a meaningful word). When other thoughts come, gently return to your word. Practice for 10-20 minutes. Best done daily, not just during pain.
