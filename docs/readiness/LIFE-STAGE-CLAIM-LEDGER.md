# Ovuluna life-stage evidence ledger

Source check: 30 September 2026. Prepared for a local draft, with clinical/editorial review pending. No production deployment, clinical certification, device validation or Apple acceptance is claimed.

## Method and boundaries

Gold Drop evidence grounding: read current primary pages, separate source dates from check dates, compare independent institutions, scope each claim to its population, and record uncertainty. Guidelines are strongest for care recommendations; their publication year is not proof of new experimental data. Two independent institutions support load-bearing educational claims where available. A guideline plus an institution patient leaflet is corroboration, not two independent clinical trials. Apple policy has only its official primary authority. WHO postnatal landing-page use is restricted to its explicit broad scope.

Worldwide copy uses local emergency services and nearest emergency departments. Country guidelines stay labelled as sources. No default UK, US or Singapore emergency number is embedded. Help lookup: https://findahelpline.com/; its availability is navigation support, not clinical triage.

The implemented data provides five optional chapters, four substantive sections per chapter, cited care alerts, and practical links. Life-stage choice is not a diagnosis or a required sequential life journey. No fertility, ovarian-age, menopause, PCOS, pulse or stress algorithm was clinically validated by this review. Personalized treatment, fasting or supplement prescriptions are outside this evidence layer.

## Contradictions, dated updates and rejected inferences

- Cycle-length conventions differ: NHS adult patient information uses21–35days; FIGO sources often use24–38days. Adolescents, postpartum, contraception and perimenopause need different context. The new copy deliberately gives no blanket normal-length classification.
- Fertility help timing differs: ASRM uses6months from age35; NHS says earlier advice from36. The app uses the broader mid-30s and individual concerns, with source-specific numbers only in the receipt.
- Natural menopause definition is not contraception clearance. WHO broad12month guidance and NHS age-specific1/2year contraception advice differ in scope. The app never instructs someone to stop contraception from a counter.
- NICE NG23 was updated15April2026. The direct page/PDF returned403; ESE2025 full page was also unavailable. Indexed first-party excerpts were leads only. Their blocked full pages are not marked verified app receipts; fully retrieved KKH2026 Annex A and Dudley guidance replace the test claim.
- ACOG published a postmenopausal-bleeding update16April2026, changing usual investigation strategy compared with its2018 ultrasound guidance. The indexed news passage was checked but full page was blocked. It is not used as a verified app source. Patient copy only requires assessment and does not choose tests.
- KKH MCHRI released Singapore Menopause Transition guidance20February2026; Annex A is fully visible in its first-party announcement. The app uses the diagnosis-context passage and labels geography. Hormone therapy remains an individual prescribing decision; no global algorithm is inferred.
- A2025 prospective wearable study (PMID39881571; DOI10.1093/humrep/deaf005) studies retrospective wrist-temperature algorithms, not Ovuluna camera pulse, ovarian age or exact prospective ovulation. The abstract alone was read; not used in verified app receipts.
- The2025 adolescent PCOS article reports the2023 international guideline with earlier evidence searches, not new2025 efficacy trials. Diagnosis requires clinical criteria/exclusions; AMH and ultrasound morphology are inappropriate adolescent diagnostic shortcuts.
- Menopause-treatment lists differ by guideline and intended outcome. NHS lists options; NICE discourages routinely using some nonhormonal medicines as first-line vasomotor care. The app offers a clinician conversation, not a drug-choice engine.
- No reviewed evidence validates a universal12-hour fasting prescription from low camera HRV/high pulse, TCM pattern diagnosis from PPG, or recovery of ovarian reserve from particular foods. Do not retain such inference elsewhere in the app merely because this layer has citations.
- Discharge colour/smell can prompt assessment but cannot establish infection, estrogen level or fertility. CDC specifically finds history alone insufficient for vaginitis diagnosis. No mucus-photo algorithm is validated.
- Source-review dates must not be confused with retrieval dates, page footers or review-due dates. NHS first periods/cycle/infertility/pregnancy-test text is older than some2026 media or footer dates; metadata preserves this.

## Apple review implications

Apple1.4.1 scrutinizes medical measurement accuracy, requires a disclosed supported methodology, and expects clinician consultation reminders. Privacy/consent/retention/deletion obligations apply, including in-app account deletion when accounts can be created. An evidence button is a useful design choice, not an explicit Apple requirement or an approval guarantee. The web app is not an accepted native submission.
