## Primary-source checks

- [Apple membership changes](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/updating-your-account-information/): founder/cofounder, D-U-N-S and possible business-document verification apply to Individual-to-Organization requests. Its separate organization-name-change warning mentions vendor-name/IDFV effects; that warning is **not** treated as proof that every conversion resets IDFV.
- [Apple organization enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment): public functional company-domain website and domain-associated work email are useful consistency checks. New-enrollment guidance does not explain the current case delay.
- [Apple D-U-N-S guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/): Apple checks legal-entity identity against D&B; general issuance/propagation guidance is not a case approval deadline.
- [Apple Sales and Trends download guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/measure-app-performance/download-and-view-reports): download by vendor and reporting period. Sales and Trends and final Payments/Financial Reports are distinct, so a bank or revenue view alone would not verify report preservation.
- [Apple Sales and Trends report availability](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/reporting/sales-and-trends-reports-availability/), read 1 October: daily reports follow the reporting day; monthly reports follow month-end by five days, generally by 8 a.m. PT. Daily/weekly/monthly retention is one year and yearly retention ten years; expired reports are not regenerated.
- [Apple financial reports](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/download-financial-reports/), read 1 October: final proceeds use Apple's fiscal calendar; reports require purchases/refunds and are available by the next fiscal month's first Friday, with ten-year retention. This timing is not the Sales and Trends calendar.
