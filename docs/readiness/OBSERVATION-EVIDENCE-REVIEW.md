# Ovuluna observation log: bounded source and account review

Reviewed 12 September 2026. Read-only inspection of the active Doubao continuation; no repository edits, service setup, account deletion, production verification or clinical approval.

## Short in-app wording

These are proposed product strings, not quotations or a validated inference model.

- **BBT:** “Take a waking temperature before getting up, eating or drinking. Record the thermometer, measurement site and time. Note fever or anything that changed your usual routine.” ACOG gives the waking/before-activity instructions; ASRM describes daily BBT as often unreliable for predicting ovarian function. Do not convert saved temperatures into an ovulation confirmation. [ACOG FAQ](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/fertility-awareness-based-methods-of-family-planning), [ASRM evaluation](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/fertility-evaluation-of-infertile-women-a-committee-opinion-2021/)
- **Mucus:** “Record what you noticed and how it felt. Choose ‘unsure’ when you cannot tell. Medicines, products, sex, bleeding and breastfeeding can affect observations.” ACOG covers appearance/sensation and several confounders; CDC independently flags discharge, irregular bleeding, medicines and postpartum context as interpretation problems. Avoid fertility labels, infection diagnoses or instructions that encourage douching. [ACOG FAQ](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/fertility-awareness-based-methods-of-family-planning), [CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usmec/fertility-awareness-based-methods.html)
- **Urine LH:** “Follow your test brand’s collection and reading instructions. Save the result exactly as shown, including invalid or unclear results.” FDA describes differences between brands; ASRM identifies urine LH as indirect evidence with possible false positives and negatives. Do not impose a universal time of day or convert a strip ratio into a concentration. [FDA home urine test](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/home-use-tests/ovulation-urine-test), [ASRM evaluation](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/fertility-evaluation-of-infertile-women-a-committee-opinion-2021/)
- **Urine PdG:** “PdG is a urine metabolite of progesterone. Keep the kit’s original result and units; it is a different measurement from blood progesterone.” FDA's Proov record identifies this exact analyte/specimen distinction. Its indication belongs to that particular test; it does not validate Ovuluna or justify transferring a generic threshold into the app. [FDA K191462](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf19/K191462.pdf), [ASRM serum-progesterone context](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/fertility-evaluation-of-infertile-women-a-committee-opinion-2021/)
- **Wearables:** “Keep sleeping wrist-temperature readings separate from waking thermometer readings. Record the device and whether its value is an absolute temperature or a change from baseline.” Apple describes nightly wrist-temperature aggregates and baseline-relative display; an external Cycle Tracking estimate is an estimate, not a raw measurement. [Apple wrist temperature](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102674), [Apple retrospective estimates](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/120357)
- **Treatment context:** “You can add medicines, treatment dates and a note from your care plan. Follow your clinician’s testing instructions.” ASRM supports history and treatment context; CDC identifies medication and reproductive-stage effects. No default assumption of ‘untreated’, automated test scheduling or advice to stop/change medication. [ASRM evaluation](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/fertility-evaluation-of-infertile-women-a-committee-opinion-2021/), [CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usmec/fertility-awareness-based-methods.html)

## Essential data and interaction decisions

The following are engineering recommendations inferred from the source distinctions, not published clinical requirements:

1. Keep observation date/time separate from creation/edit timestamps. Preserve local calendar dates without UTC day-shift; allow unknown time. A manually copied device/lab/clinician result remains user-transcribed.
2. Keep BBT, absolute wrist temperature, wrist change-from-baseline, urine LH, urine PdG, serum progesterone and external app estimates distinguishable. Do not silently infer missing units, method, specimen or provenance. No automatic body-temperature conversion for a baseline delta: Fahrenheit deltas scale by 5/9 without subtracting 32.
3. Missing, not observed, unsure, invalid and negative are different states. Avoid preset ‘normal’, numeric zero, default cycle day or an unchecked treatment flag becoming ‘no treatment’.
4. If allowing qualitative urine results, ‘positive/negative’ must be the user's reading of the kit instructions. Some PdG competitive assays use inverse line logic: Proov's FDA summary has one line positive/two lines negative. Do not reuse an LH strip image classifier or generic two-line-positive help.
5. Keep original numeric value/unit and any original qualifier. The FDA's general LH consumer page describes qualitative kits; it should not be read as a claim that every modern monitor is qualitative. Capture the actual brand/model and result format.
6. Save/edit/remove actions must show real persistence outcomes, preserve unsaved input on failure, reject stale records after cross-tab changes/deletion, and never recreate a deleted record from a stale edit. Render free text as text; CSV exports need formula-injection handling if offered.
7. Export actual observations and known gaps with sources/timestamps; no inferred fertile days, diagnoses, ovarian age, confidence score or fasting rule. An export is a local download, not transmission to a clinician. An omitted observation is not evidence of a negative finding.
8. Add the exact new storage key to the explicit Settings inventory, raw export and reviewed deletion. Preserve unknown/malformed records and unknown stores; don't treat a parse failure as permission to overwrite. Protect draft/state from stale-tab restoration. No intimate photo collection is needed for this feature.

## Current account availability: code verified

- `settings.html` links Delete my data to `privacy.html#privacy-local-title` and Delete my account to `privacy.html#privacy-account-title`; it declares no connected account, sign-in, cloud backup or device sync.
- `privacy.html` explicitly cannot look up/delete server accounts. It distinguishes local records from other installations, downloaded exports, external services and external subscriptions.
- `privacy-local.js` recognizes an explicit key list plus dated legacy keys. Export preserves raw strings. Removal checks the reviewed raw values, then reports per-key failures; storage events invalidate a review. It has no account API call. This is origin-scoped localStorage, not atomic multi-tab storage or verified account erasure.
- `login.html` actions navigate to Home or onboarding. `api/` lists AI/NFC/food endpoints and helper files, no signup/session/deletion endpoint. `api/_lib/security.mjs` provides an optional trusted introspection boundary and fails closed when configuration is missing; it does not issue identity or provide account lifecycle.
- Therefore a working server-account deletion button cannot truthfully be claimed in this checked-out build. No deployed credentials/settings were inspected, and absence of a remote service outside this repository was not established.
