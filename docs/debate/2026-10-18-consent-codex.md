**1. Problems with the current text**

This reviews the supplied facts, not verified code or provider contracts. Store rules apply when distributed through those stores.

- **Today:** it omits China, the third-party AI service, 7/28-day summaries and onboarding disclosures, premium-code records, hosting logs and Google Fonts. Removing names does not establish anonymity. “Nothing has been saved yet” overlooks connection logs.
- **Future:** “saved on this phone” becomes incomplete with cloud storage. Specify locations, recipients, purposes, retention, backups and deletion before uploading. Product improvement cannot authorise unspecified research.
- **Promises:** verify provider retention, training and human-access practices before asserting “nothing stored” or “nobody browses.” No-sale/no-advertising commitments need enforceable controls.
- **PDPA:** complete the linked policy before seeking consent. Identify the operator and DPO contact; explain purposes, access/correction, withdrawal and retention. Withdrawal stops consent-based future processing, not necessarily lawful retention. Health data needs stronger safeguards, although PDPA has no GDPR-style special-category regime. Consent cannot replace security. [PDPC healthcare guidance](https://files.app.optical.gov.sg/pdpc/production/assets/a5c6e9b3-c870-4ba0-a0a0-9774b1d90e7a.pdf), [health-data safeguards](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/resource-for-organisation/2020-personal-data-protection-digest.pdf).
- **Minors:** 16 is not Singapore’s general consent threshold. Sufficiently understanding 13–17-year-olds may consent; below 13 requires parental/guardian consent. Merely advising parental help is insufficient. Gate health questions appropriately. [PDPC children’s guidance](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/advisory-guidelines/advisory-guidelines-on-the-pdpa-for-children%27s-personal-data-in-the-digital-environment_mar24.pdf).
- **Stores:** Apple requires consent, withdrawal, minimisation and explicit third-party AI permission; health-data mining has restricted purposes. Google requires health declarations, accurate Data safety disclosures, a public policy and applicable medical disclaimers. Human-subject health research requires specific consent and ethics review, subject to Google’s exemption provision. [Apple 5.1.1–5.1.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy), [Google health policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511?hl=en-GB).

**2. Optional purposes and pre-ticks**

Keep AI, pooled improvement, research and future sync optional to today’s local diary. Necessary processing can be required for a chosen feature; unnecessary onboarding fields should be skippable.

Singapore does not categorically invalidate every pre-tick, but PDPC recommends explicit action. **Do not pre-tick these choices.** Apple requires permission even for anonymous collection; Google requires affirmative consent where its prominent-disclosure rule applies. Bundled acceptance is inadequate for these poorly disclosed flows. [PDPC design guidance](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/Files/PDPC/PDF-Files/Other-Guides/Guide-to-Data-Protection-by-Design-for-ICT-Systems-%28310519%29.pdf?la=en), [Google User Data](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).

Genuinely anonymised outputs fall outside PDPA; producing them from personal records still needs a lawful basis. Exceptions require assessment. Generative-model training needs AI-specific notification, not vague improvement wording. [PDPC July 2026 guidance](https://files.app.optical.gov.sg/pdpc/production/assets/143cb9d4-532e-4cca-9a77-bcc0415ca294.pdf).

**3. Overseas AI without vendor branding**

Use cards 3–4 below. Model names need not appear. Concealing the legal recipient everywhere cannot be guaranteed store-compliant; identify it in accessible policy details before permission.

China processing is not automatically prohibited. Establish compliant transfer safeguards, normally enforceable comparable protection. If relying on transfer consent, provide the required written protection summary. [Transfer regulations](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/PDPA2012-S63-2021#pr10-).

**4. Full replacement**

*Use only after implementing these controls and completing the policy.*

**Title:** Your health notes, your choices  
**Sub:** Start with a diary on this phone. Choose separately whether Luna can use AI.

**Card 1: Your records**  
Your chosen periods, symptoms, pain, mood, sleep, meals, photos, weight, mindfulness minutes and answers about pregnancy, hormones, medicines, diagnoses, family history and body signals help show your cycle and patterns. We don’t sell your data or use it for ads.

**Card 2: Saved on this phone**  
Your diary is stored in this browser, without an account or cloud backup. Clearing browser data or losing your phone may lose your records.

**Card 3: Optional AI help**  
When enabled, notes the phone cannot understand and food photos pass through Rhythme’s server to a third-party AI service in China. This helps Luna turn them into entries.

**Card 4: Optional journal readings**  
Readings send a 7- or 28-day summary, including entries, cycle day and onboarding answers, through that same service. We attach no name, but sent content may identify you.

**Card 5: Service information**  
GitHub hosting keeps connection logs, and Google receives connection information when fonts load. Redeeming a premium code records its use and a hashed network address to prevent misuse.

**Card 6: Your controls**  
Switch AI off in Luna’s settings to stop future AI sends. Export or delete your diary from You; diary deletion does not erase service logs.

**Card 7: Wellbeing, with limits**  
Predictions and AI can be wrong; Rhythme does not diagnose, treat or provide contraception. Seek professional advice for health concerns; in a Singapore emergency, call 995.

**Choices**

- Required, unchecked: Use my entries on this phone for diary and cycle features.
- Optional, OFF: Allow Luna’s note/photo AI processing in China.
- Optional, OFF: Allow journal-summary AI processing in China.
- Improvement/research: not offered today; seek separate, specific permissions when introduced.

**Under-16 note:** Under 16? Read this with a parent or guardian. Under 13? A parent or guardian must complete consent before health questions.
