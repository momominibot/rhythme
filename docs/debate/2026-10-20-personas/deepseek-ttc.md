## 2. Safety red flags and exact wording

Clinical benchmark: for a woman under 35, evaluation is usually offered after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse; over 35 after 6 months (NICE CG156 https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg156; ACOG https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/infertility-evaluation-and-treatment).

Exact wording to show Wei Ling when relevant logs appear:

"Call 995 or go to Emergency now for severe one-sided pelvic pain, shoulder tip pain, fainting, or bleeding after a missed period."

"See a doctor soon at a polyclinic, KKH or NUH for bleeding between periods, pain during sex, sudden change in periods, or pelvic pain that does not settle with usual pain relief."

"Call SOS 1767 if you feel hopeless, unsafe or think about harming yourself. Text mindline 1771 for mental health support."

"After 12 months of regular trying, a fertility check is sensible. Eight months is not a sign of failure."

## 4. Clinically wrong, culturally off, or likely making me stop

- **The app is too quiet on red flags for TTC.** A 33-year-old who has been trying 8 months does not need disease framing, but the period and pain logger must escalate severe pain, bleeding between periods, pain during sex or fainting. Without this, it feels unsafe.
- **The stress scan is misleading.** The inventory says a Learn link is labelled PSS-10 but opens camera scanning, not a validated questionnaire. I would stop using anything that presents experimental heart-rate or face-camera output as stress insight without a clear "experimental" label.
- **The Everyday confirmation "Did you really do it, with attention?" is off.** For someone anxious about failing, this adds performance pressure. Mindfulness should invite noticing, not check compliance.
- **Herb/tea and acupressure sections are culturally easy to accept in Singapore but clinically unverified.** The app should label them as complementary and say "Ask your doctor", especially in a TTC context where people may self-treat.
- **MEDAS14, labs, hydration, face camera and legacy sleep data are separate stores.** This fragments the record and may mislead Wei Ling into thinking she has a complete clinical picture when she does not.
- **Journal reflection and imports bypass afterSave.** This breaks the owner requirement that hearts are earned for logging data only and makes rewards inconsistent.
- **The fertile window is not explicit enough in the calendar or period details.** For TTC, that is the main thing she wants; the app would make me stop if it logs periods without helping her understand her own cycle context.
