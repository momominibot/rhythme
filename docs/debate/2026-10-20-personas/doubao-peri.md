## 2. Safety escalations for Siti

Clinical basis: NICE and ACOG advise urgent assessment for very heavy bleeding, dizziness, fainting, chest pain or breathing difficulty; irregular, prolonged or postmenopausal bleeding should also be assessed. Sources are listed below.

**If she logs soaking a pad or tampon hourly for 2 or more hours, flooding, large clots, dizziness, fainting, weakness or breathlessness:**

> “Please call 995 now or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not drive if you feel faint. Tell them how much you have bled and that you take amlodipine.”

**If she logs chest pain, severe breathlessness, severe headache, one-sided weakness, facial droop or trouble speaking:**

> “Call 995 now. Do not wait for another reading or reply from the app.”

**If she logs heavy or persistent bleeding, bleeding after sex, bleeding that disrupts work or sleep, or bleeding after 12 months without a period:**

> “Please arrange a GP, polyclinic, KKH or NUH review soon. Bleeding like this should be assessed.”

**If pregnancy is possible and she has pain, bleeding, dizziness or shoulder pain:**

> “Take a pregnancy test. If it is positive with pain or bleeding, call 995 or go to emergency care now.”

**If she expresses thoughts of self-harm, hopelessness or feeling unable to stay safe:**

> “Please call SOS 1767 now. Call 995 if you think you may hurt yourself. mindline 1771 is also available.”

**For herbs, supplements or formulas viewed while amlodipine is recorded:**

> “Check with your doctor or pharmacist before use. Do not change your blood pressure medicine yourself.”

2. **Hot flush and night-sweat tracking:** Add a quick flush button at **Wake up** and **Bedtime**. Show: “Night flushes can be hard. Notice one breath, then record.”
3. **Universal post-save experience:** After every confirmed save, import, undo or removal, show the outfit-aware avatar, one mindful line and consistent reward feedback.

6. **Relabel camera tools:** In **Face-camera Calm check**, **Finger-camera pulse** and **Legacy Stress scan**, show: “Experimental. This is not a diagnosis or blood pressure reading.”

8. **Herb safety:** In **Greenhouse teas/herbs** and **Food/herb/formula catalogue**, show the doctor or pharmacist caution when amlodipine is recorded.
9. **Reward clarity:** Hearts are for logged data only; mindfulness gives one small flat practice amount. Remove conflicting reward text in **Everyday practice selection and confirmation**.

## 4. Clinically wrong, misleading or culturally off

- **Food: Ideas**, **Greenhouse teas/herbs** and phase filters in **Food/herb/formula catalogue** should not assume a clear cycle phase during perimenopause.
- Herb or formula suggestions targeted at symptoms could interact with amlodipine. The HSA advises caution with health products and supplements.
- **Face-camera Calm check**, **Finger-camera pulse** and **Legacy Stress scan** must not imply diagnosis of stress, blood pressure or wellbeing.
- The PSS-10-labelled route to a camera scan in **Legacy Stress scan** is misleading.
- **Medicines/products stack** appearing to record amlodipine without persistence is a safety problem.
- **Farm well** saying the can is full without changing state is misleading.
- **Luna journal readings** cannot be treated as a reliable clinical or mindfulness cue because wording is generated.
- **Acupressure** is acceptable only as gentle self-care with “stop if it hurts”, never as a reason to delay heavy-bleeding care.
- MEDAS14 may feel culturally distant because it is built around a Mediterranean diet. It should say it is only a short dietary pattern quiz, not a health score. Local foods can be added later without changing today’s safety requirement.
