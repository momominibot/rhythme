## 2. Safety escalations for Mrs Tan

Exact wording I would want shown:

- Postmenopausal bleeding: "This bleeding is not a period. Contact a polyclinic, KKH, NUH or your doctor today. If you have heavy bleeding, pain, faintness or chest pain, call 995 now."  
  Source: NICE NG12 `https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng12`; RCOG `https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/browse-all-patient-information-leaflets/postmenopausal-bleeding/`; ACOG `https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/postmenopausal-bleeding`.
- Severe low mood or self-harm: "If you might harm yourself, call SOS 1767 now. If you are in immediate danger, call 995. For non urgent support, try mindline.sg 1771."  
  Singapore context: `https://www.sos.org.sg`; `https://www.mindline.sg`.
- Chest pain, breathlessness, fainting, weakness on one side: "Call 995 now. These symptoms need urgent care."
- New breast lump, unexplained weight loss, persistent bloating: "See your doctor soon. This needs checking."  
  NICE NG12 same URL.
- Poor sleep with snoring, gasping or stopping breathing: "See your polyclinic doctor. Your sleep may need assessment."
- Osteopenia or fall with hip or back pain: "See your doctor. Do not start bone supplements without advice."  
  HealthHub osteoporosis `https://www.healthhub.sg/a-z/diseases-and-conditions/osteoporosis`.

## 3. Top ten fixes

1. Fix the Mind-route visibility bug so the normal avatar is unhidden before assigning everyday practice poses. She must stay visible in her own outfit.
2. Add a safety card to any bleeding, period or symptom entry for users over 45 or more than 12 months past periods. Exact wording: "This bleeding is not a period. Contact a polyclinic, KKH, NUH or your doctor today."
3. Stop calendar quick logging and period autofill from bypassing `afterSave`. Postmenopausal bleeding must never be saved as a period silently.
4. Add the mindful line from Section 1 before every save as a small non intrusive toast. No streaks, no guilt.
5. Add an avatar action for every activity using the current outfit unless the real world activity truly requires a change.
6. Create outfit variants for meditation, shower, stretch and sleeping. Fixed base or pajama poses break the requirement.
7. Change the five minute orb tile from "silent" to "without voice" so the chime does not mislead.
8. Make farm watering, weeding, planting and harvesting use the same real world confirmation as garden watering: "I did it with attention" before expending energy.
9. Route imports, journal reflections, hydration, MEDAS14, CSV and lab records through the same reflection and heart feedback as manual saves.
10. Mark face-camera Calm check, finger-camera pulse and legacy stress scan as "experimental wellness estimate; not a medical reading".

## 4. Clinically wrong, culturally off, or would make me stop

Treating postmenopausal bleeding as a cycle is clinically wrong. The inventory shows "Calendar and quick period logging" and "Period details" still use period language and quick additions. At 54, any bleeding must be escalated. I would stop using the app if it allowed me to save bleeding as a period without a prominent warning. Sources: NICE NG12 `https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng12`; KKH `https://www.kkh.com.sg/patient-care/conditions-treatments/postmenopausal-bleeding`; NUH `https://www.nuh.com.sg/care-at-nuh/specialties/womens-health/menopause`.

Experimental camera pulse readings are misleading for me. Face-camera Calm check, finger-camera pulse and legacy Stress scan are not validated diagnostic tools. They may cause health anxiety. Label them unverified.

The food/herb/formula catalogue and greenhouse teas are culturally relevant in Singapore but risk implying menopause treatment. Every entry needs: "Not medical advice. Check with your doctor or pharmacist." Unverified.

The Medicines/products stack adds rows without persistence. For a 54 year old managing medicines, this is unsafe. It must save and warn to confirm with a pharmacist.

Acupressure is presented with evidence wording. I would want only: "Some people find comfort; evidence is limited. Stop if anything hurts." Otherwise it feels misleading.

The social features are sample neighbours and fixed phrases. As a retired person using the app for company, this could feel hollow or performative. I would stop if friendship felt like a chore or a game score.

Finally, hearts are earned for logging data only. That is acceptable only if practice still earns a small flat amount and no activity becomes a chore. The garden and farm must not make me farm to feel good.
