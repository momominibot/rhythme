- **listening:** seated, head slightly tilted, hands resting.
- **throw:** arm drawn back, eyes following an imagined ball.
- **bounce:** rises on toes, arms slightly out, smiling.

# 2. Safety escalations

These should interrupt the relevant activity, especially Calendar and quick period logging, Period details, Symptoms and mood, and Luna text logging.

- **Any postmenopausal bleeding:** “Any bleeding after your periods have stopped needs medical assessment. Please see a doctor today, even if it is only spotting.”
- **Heavy bleeding or collapse:** “Call 995 now if bleeding soaks a pad in an hour, you feel faint, or you have severe pain.”
- **Self-harm or suicide thoughts:** “If you may hurt yourself or do not want to live, call SOS 1767 now. Call 995 if danger feels immediate.”
- **Other emergency symptoms:** “Call 995 now for chest pain, breathlessness, fainting, sudden weakness, speech trouble, or a sudden severe headache.”
- **Severe abdominal or pelvic symptoms:** “Seek urgent care now for severe abdominal or pelvic pain, fever, or repeated vomiting.”
- **Possible fracture after a fall:** “After a fall, seek urgent care for severe back or hip pain or inability to bear weight.”
- **Emotional support:** “For a confidential chat, call or text mindline at 1771.”
- **Non-urgent menopause concerns:** “For dryness, pain, urinary symptoms, or poor sleep, book a polyclinic appointment or ask for KKH or NUH care.”

Clinical basis: postmenopausal bleeding should be evaluated, and menopause is confirmed after 12 months without a period. Sources: [ACOG, Bleeding After Menopause](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/bleeding-after-menopause) and [ACOG, Menopause](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/menopause).

# 4. Clinical, cultural and trust concerns

- The app must not frame postmenopausal bleeding as a normal period. This is the most serious issue in the inventory. Source: [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/bleeding-after-menopause).
- Phase based food and herb suggestions are not meaningful two years after the final period. Use general menopause, bone, heart and energy categories instead. Source: [ACOG, Menopause](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/menopause).
- For osteopenia, stretching alone is not enough. Older adults benefit from muscle strengthening, balance and safe weight bearing activity. Source: [WHO Physical Activity](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity).
- MEDAS14 measures adherence to a Mediterranean diet. It may fit Singapore hawker and mixed Asian food poorly, so present it as a learning score, not a health rating. This local applicability is **unverified**.
- The herb and formula catalogue should tell users to ask a doctor or pharmacist about interactions with blood thinners, sedatives, cancer treatment or other medicines. Regulatory information: [Health Sciences Authority](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/).
- I would stop trusting the app if medicines appeared saved but were not, if the Farm well lied about refill, or if camera scans were presented as diagnoses.
- The Legacy Stress scan must not be labelled PSS-10 when it actually runs a camera scan.
- The “silent” five minute orb should not chime, or the tile should say “guided with chimes.”
- Sample neighbours must always be clearly labelled so the plaza does not feel like fake company.
- The podcast publication link should open the promised episode post, not a general publication page.
