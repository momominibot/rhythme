# 2. Safety escalations

- **Possible ectopic pregnancy warning:** “If you might be pregnant and have severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, dizziness, fainting, or shoulder pain, call 995 now or go to KKH or NUH Emergency.”
- **Heavy bleeding warning:** “If you soak through a pad or tampon every hour for 2 or more hours, pass large clots, or feel faint, call 995 or go to the emergency department now.”
- **Pregnancy bleeding or pain:** “If your pregnancy test is positive and you bleed or have pain, contact KKH, NUH, a polyclinic doctor or your own doctor today. Call 995 for severe symptoms.”
- **Pelvic infection or sudden severe pain:** “Severe pelvic pain with fever, vomiting or unusual discharge needs urgent medical care today.”
- **Mental health crisis:** “If you might hurt yourself, call SOS 1767 now. Call 995 if you feel unsafe. You can also use mindline 1771.”
- **Camera pulse readings:** “Do not use this experimental reading for chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or sustained palpitations. Call 995.”
- **Fertility timing support:** “At 33 after 8 months, you are not failing. Fertility review is commonly considered after 12 months under age 35, or earlier if you have pain, irregular cycles or concerns.”

Clinical basis: ACOG infertility guidance, ACOG ectopic pregnancy guidance, ACOG heavy menstrual bleeding guidance, and WHO suicide resources. Singapore services: **995**, **SOS 1767**, **mindline 1771**, **KKH**, **NUH** and polyclinics.

# 4. Clinical, cultural and trust concerns

- The **Legacy Stress scan** is labelled PSS-10 but actually runs a camera scan. This is clinically misleading and should be renamed immediately.
- **Face-camera Calm check**, **Finger-camera pulse** and **Legacy Stress scan** must be labelled experimental and not used to diagnose stress, calmness or heart problems.
- App fertile-day predictions should not be presented as guaranteed ovulation. ACOG states the fertile window includes the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation, and fertility awareness methods depend on careful biological signs, not past dates alone.
- At age 33 after eight months of trying, Wei Ling does not meet the common under-35 infertility review threshold of 12 months. The app should avoid implying failure. ACOG: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/infertility
- Severe one-sided pain, shoulder pain, fainting or heavy bleeding in possible pregnancy can indicate ectopic pregnancy and requires emergency care. ACOG: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/ectopic-pregnancy
- Heavy bleeding causing hourly pad or tampon changes, large clots or faintness needs urgent assessment. ACOG: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/heavy-menstrual-bleeding
- **Acupressure** and phase-sorted **Greenhouse teas/herbs** should carry an unverified, non-medical label and advise checking with a clinician during pregnancy or while trying to conceive. They should not claim to improve fertility.
- Self-harm escalation should follow WHO crisis guidance and provide SOS 1767, mindline 1771 and 995. WHO: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/suicide
- I see no obvious racial or religious insult in the inventory, but Singaporean trust requires local care pathways: 995, polyclinics, KKH and NUH, rather than vague foreign advice.
- I would stop using the app if it gave a diagnosis from a camera scan, promised herbal fertility benefits, or made eight months of trying feel like failure.
