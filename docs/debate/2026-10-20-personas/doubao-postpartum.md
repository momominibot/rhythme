## 2. Safety escalations and exact wording

- **Self-harm, psychosis, or unsafe care:**  
  “Please call 995 now if you might hurt yourself or your baby, or if you see or hear things others do not. If you feel unsafe, call SOS 1767 or mindline 1771. KKH and NUH emergency care are available.”

- **Persistent low mood or anxiety:**  
  “If sadness, anxiety, or numbness lasts most days for two weeks or makes care hard, please speak to a polyclinic doctor, KKH, or NUH. For support tonight, call SOS 1767 or mindline 1771.”

- **Heavy vaginal bleeding:**  
  “Please seek urgent care now if you soak a pad in one hour, pass large clots, feel faint, or cannot manage at home. Call 995 or go to KKH or NUH Accident and Emergency.”

- **Breast infection warning signs:**  
  “For a painful red breast, fever, or flu-like aches, see a doctor today at a polyclinic, KKH, or NUH. Do not stop feeding suddenly without advice.”

- **Postpartum physical emergency:**  
  “Call 995 now for chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden severe headache, vision changes, fever, wound pus, a swollen red calf, or severe abdominal pain.”

- **Broken sleep and safety:**  
  “If you feel confused, unsafe, or unable to care for your baby because of sleep loss, call 995 or go to KKH or NUH now.”

- **Fertility while breastfeeding:**  
  “Breastfeeding and absent periods do not guarantee pregnancy prevention. If you could be pregnant or need contraception, ask a polyclinic, KKH, or NUH doctor.”

## 3. Top ten fixes, ranked

1. **Add a postpartum profile state.** Onboarding should say: “I am postpartum and breastfeeding, and my periods may not be back.” Hide phase-based suggestions until real cycle data exists.
2. **Fix Mind visibility.** Every Mind route must unhide the normal avatar before assigning a pose, so she is visibly acting in her own outfit.
3. **Create outfit-aware meditation, shower, and stretch art.** Keep her tudung and selected outfit, or use a modest tudung-friendly variant.
4. **Add one short breath line and pose to every activity.** It should appear automatically without an extra form, confirmation, streak, or score.
5. **Make sleep logging postpartum-friendly.** Say “broken night,” allow multiple wake entries, and avoid wording that suggests fragmented sleep is failure.
6. **Neutralise food support.** Add a halal preference, avoid pork and alcohol suggestions, remove calorie-led judgment, and disable phase-based food ideas.
7. **Standardise practice rewards.** Show: “Mindful practice complete. +5 hearts.” Give the same small flat amount for every practice, with no streak or pressure.
8. **Relabel experimental camera tools.** Say: “Experimental only. This is not a medical diagnosis.” Rename the PSS-10 link because it currently opens a camera scan.
9. **Fix misleading utilities.** Either persist medicines properly or remove the stack; make the farm well change state; make publication links match the episode.
10. **Strengthen social safety.** Label sample neighbours clearly, provide one-tap report and block, and ensure turning Plaza friends off removes card, friendships, and notes.

## 4. Clinical, cultural, and trust concerns

- Periods can remain absent during breastfeeding, but ovulation may happen before the first period. The app must not treat the calendar as a fertility guarantee. Source: ACOG, Postpartum Contraception, https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/postpartum-contraception
- Persistent low mood, anxiety, numbness, thoughts of self-harm, or difficulty caring for the baby requires clinical escalation. Source: ACOG, Depression During Pregnancy and After Pregnancy, https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/depression-during-pregnancy-and-after-pregnancy
- Heavy bleeding, chest pain, breathlessness, severe headache, vision changes, fever, wound infection, or calf swelling require urgent assessment. Source: ACOG, Optimizing Postpartum Care, https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2018/09/optimizing-postpartum-care
- Breastfeeding support should be cautious with herbs, supplements, and formulas because safety is not universally established. The catalogue should require clinician advice rather than implying phase-based benefit. WHO breastfeeding overview: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/breastfeeding
- I would stop using the app if it replaced my tudung with fixed yoga, meditation, or shower art, hid crisis support, called an experimental camera scan a stress test, or let me believe non-saved medicines were recorded.
- The availability of Plaza friends, backend calls, and Luna-generated readings is **unverified** from the inventory and should not be presented as reliable clinical or social features.
