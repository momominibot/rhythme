2. Fever with pelvic pain card under-triages pregnancy and recent birth  
Exact current text: "A fever together with pelvic or tummy pain, an unusual discharge, a hot painful breast, a sore wound or painful peeing needs a doctor today. See your GP or polyclinic today. Go to A&E, or KKH’s 24-hour Urgent O&G Centre, if you feel very unwell, faint or confused."  
Replacement: "A fever together with pelvic or tummy pain, an unusual discharge, a hot painful breast, a sore wound or painful peeing needs a doctor today. If you could be pregnant or recently gave birth, go to KKH’s 24-hour Urgent O&G Centre or an A&E now. Otherwise see your GP or polyclinic today. Go to A&E, or KKH’s 24-hour Urgent O&G Centre, if you feel very unwell, faint or confused."  
Source: https://www.rcog.org.uk/guidance/browse-all-guidance/green-top-guidelines/bacterial-sepsis-in-and-following-pregnancy-green-top-guideline-no-64a/
