## 2. Safety red flags and exact wording

Clinical sources: heavy bleeding red flags (NICE NG88 https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng88, ACOG https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/perimenopausal-bleeding-and-bleeding-after-menopause), high blood pressure emergency signs (HealthHub Singapore https://www.healthhub.sg/a-z/diseases-and-conditions/hypertension). The camera pulse and stress scans are unverified and should be labelled experimental.

Show these exact lines to Siti:

- “Heavy bleeding that soaks through a pad or tampon every hour for more than 2 hours. Call 995 or go to the nearest emergency department.”
- “Heavy bleeding with faintness, dizziness, chest pain, or shortness of breath. Call 995 now.”
- “New bleeding after sex or between periods. See a polyclinic doctor or gynaecologist within 2 weeks.”
- “Period bleeding that stops you leaving home, work, or your shift, or large clots. Ask your doctor for review.”
- “Severe headache, blurred vision, chest pain, confusion, weakness one side, or fainting. Call 995 now.”
- “Do not stop amlodipine on your own. Swollen ankles, dizziness, or palpitations need a polyclinic review.”
- “Fever, drenching night sweats with weight loss, or a breast lump. See a doctor within 2 weeks.”
- “If you feel unsafe or have thoughts of harming yourself, call SOS 1767 or mindline 1771, or 995 if in immediate danger.”
- “Ask your polyclinic doctor for referral to KKH or NUH gynaecology if heavy bleeding continues.”

## 4. Wrong, culturally off, or likely to make me stop

The camera-based Face-camera Calm check, Finger-camera pulse, and Legacy Stress scan are clinically unsafe without clear validation. Siti has high blood pressure and could be falsely reassured or frightened. “Legacy Stress scan” is especially misleading because the PSS-10 label opens an rPPG camera scan, not the PSS-10 questionnaire. I would stop using any feature that presents these as health monitoring.

The Greenhouse teas/herbs, Food/herb/formula catalogue, and acupressure evidence sections may feel like treatment advice. For a Singapore woman on amlodipine, herbs or supplements can interact with blood pressure medicine. The inventory does not show a drug interaction check. Unverified herbal advice is unsafe and could make me stop using the app.

The Medicines/products stack is not persisted. Siti needs a reliable medicine list for amlodipine. A visual stack that disappears or does not save is dangerous for medication tracking.

The food ideas and search are phase-based but the inventory does not show local Singapore food names, halal filters, or shift-worker meal guidance. For a Malay woman working shifts, local hawker meals and prayer meal timing matter culturally. This gap may make food logging feel irrelevant.

Legacy sleep notes, old stress pages, and separate stores such as MEDAS14, hydration, face-camera, and labs create a broken record. Siti wants to know when to see a doctor, but fragmented stores and bypassed `afterSave` mean the doctor share may not contain everything. This is unsafe for perimenopause care.

The app currently treats weight logging as a direct save with no pause. For Siti in perimenopause, weight can be emotionally loaded and is not the main goal. A body-neutral pause is necessary.

Shift work is not explicitly supported in reminders, sleep, or window guidance. Siti needs non-preachy help around rotating shifts, night flushes, and sleep recovery. The current generic rest cues miss this context.
