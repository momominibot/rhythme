## 1. Problems with the current text

- "Your diary is saved on this phone" is true today but becomes false once cloud backup and sync start. The notice must be versioned and replaced before that change.
- "Without your name" is too reassuring. Notes, food photos and journal summaries can contain health details, and they are sent to an AI service outside Singapore. The current text hides the overseas transfer and third party processing.
- "Nobody at Rhythme browses your diary" is a risky absolute promise. Once data is stored on servers, staff may need access for support, security or debugging. A safer promise is: "We do not read your diary for ads or selling; access is limited and logged."
- "Help make Rhythme better... never your name or the words in your diary" is misleading if predictions are improved from journal entries. The words are processed even if they are not quoted later.
- "De-identified" is not the same as anonymous under the PDPA. If patterns can be linked to onboarding answers, cycle dates, or other details, they are still personal data and need consent.
- There is no mention of premium code logging, GitHub Pages server logs, Google Fonts requests, or the planned collection and storage on Rhythme servers.
- Health data is sensitive. PDPC expects prominent, specific consent for it, not a general notice buried in cards.
- Purpose limitation is weak: "possibly research later" is not in the notice. Future research needs fresh consent.
- Withdrawal is not described. PDPA gives users the right to withdraw consent; deletion is not the same as withdrawal.
- "Read with a parent or guardian" is not parental consent. The under-13 setup needs a parent to agree; 13 to 16 needs guardian involvement.
- Apple App Store 5.1.1 to 5.1.3 and Google Play health rules require clear disclosure for health data, no advertising use, and affirmative consent. A pre-ticked box is not affirmative consent.

## 2. Optional features and pre-ticking

These must be optional, not conditions of using the app:

- Luna reading, because it sends health content to a third party outside Singapore. It should be off by default.
- "Help make Rhythme better" and any research, because improvement is not needed for the diary to work.
- Cloud backup and sync, when they arrive. A user should be able to keep using the app locally without cloud storage.

Pre-ticked is not acceptable. Under PDPC guidance, consent must be active, informed and unambiguous. For sensitive health data, pre-ticking is close to opt-out consent. Apple and Google also expect a clear affirmative choice for non-core data uses. All optional choices should start unchecked and be changeable later.

## 3. Describing overseas AI processing

"To understand a note, food photo or journal summary, Rhythme sends that item to a helper service, then to an AI service provided by a third party outside Singapore. No account name is attached, but the item itself may contain health details. The service is contractually allowed to process it only to create the reply and is instructed not to keep it. You can switch Luna off."

This is honest, does not require naming the vendor, and says where the data goes without claiming it is anonymous.

## 4. Replacement first-launch notice

Title: Before your room opens

Sub: What Rhythme keeps, why, and how you stay in control.

1. What Rhythme keeps
What you log: periods, symptoms, sleep, meals and food photos, mood, calm minutes, and your getting-to-know-you answers. Today, those stay in this phone's browser. When cloud backup arrives, we will show a new notice and ask again.

2. Luna and your data
If you switch Luna on, a note, food photo, or short summary of your recent diary entries is sent to Rhythme, then to a third party AI service outside Singapore. No name is attached, but the content may include health details. It is used only to make the reply and is not kept by us.

3. Why we do this
To predict your cycle, notice patterns, and help Luna write useful readings from what you choose to share.

4. How we look after it
We do not sell your data, show ads, or let anyone at Rhythme read your diary. Access is limited to people who need it and is logged.

5. A friend, not a doctor
Rhythme helps you notice patterns. It does not diagnose and never replaces care. In an emergency, call 995.

6. Your choice, any time
Switch Luna off in settings, export your records, or delete them. You can also withdraw consent by writing to us.

7. Help improve Rhythme
If you choose, we may use patterns that do not identify you to improve predictions. We will not use your name, diary text, or food photos for this. Future research will ask for fresh consent.

Choices (both off by default):
[ ] Turn Luna on for my notes, food photos and journal summaries
[ ] Help make Rhythme better with de-identified patterns

Under 16? Please read this with a parent or guardian, and have them agree to these choices. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must set Rhythme up with you and agree to these choices.

Button: I agree, open my room

Small print: By tapping, you agree to the current Rhythme privacy notice and terms. Rhythme needs your agreement to work, so the room stays closed until then. You can change your choices later. No diary data has been saved yet. If our data practices change, we will update this notice and ask again.

## 5. Three things the app must never say

1. "Your data is anonymous." De-identified is not anonymous if it can be linked back.
2. "We never see your data." Data passes through Rhythme servers and the AI processor; say access is limited and logged.
3. "Your data is 100% secure." No absolute security; say encrypted, access controlled, and protected.
