None of these core interactions inherently requires generative AI. Models should not determine authoritative dates, recurrence counts, clinical flags or permission to mutate records.

**What must survive**

Preserve the room and mindfulness practices, while offering chat as another doorway. Keep the intake’s age, menarche, pregnancy, hormonal treatment, reproductive history and urgent-symptom coverage (`room.js:1184–1201`). It can be staged without deleting those distinctions.

Preserve `normal/wide/suspended`, deterministic flags, confirmation before saving, report coverage and model basis/limits. Ebb 4a’s “I’ve logged heavy flow and dizziness” should not override confirmation.

These are architectural strengths, not clinical validation. Current implementation has important defects:

- Reproduced: spotting starts an episode; a second same-day symptom entry replaces earlier symptoms and pain (`ovu-store.js:86–98/:137`).
- Reproduced: Ebb’s exact emergency message becomes a **lunch entry** locally (`ovu-orb.js:49–66`).
- Reproduced: onboarding saves `lifeStage: 'post'`, while the postmenopausal-bleeding rule expects `'postmenopause'` (`room.js:1278`; `ovu-agents.js:54`).
- `wide` changes uncertainty wording, not the prediction formula; phase estimation still falls back to 28 days (`ovu-store.js:161–172`).

**Copy and safety corrections**

**1c: “read your heart-rate variability, stress and recovery” and “see where you are in your cycle”.** Camera variability measurement is feasible with particular validated systems; that does not validate Ovumind’s implementation or its clinical interpretation. Existing [camera-validation research](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2025.1707318/full) is device-, population- and protocol-specific.

Require app-specific ECG agreement, failure rates across phones, lighting and skin tones, then prospective hormone-referenced cycle validation against a date-only baseline. Honest wording: **“Optional experimental camera pulse-variability reading. It does not establish cycle phase, ovulation, stress or recovery.”** Remove Ovumind’s unsourced calm formula and “healthy variability” interpretation too (`face-ppg.html:115–117`).

**3b: anxiety explained through a 12% HRV dip.** This supplies an explanation the evidence cannot establish. An original study found typical luteal HRV decreases were not associated with premenstrual emotional changes. Use **“You reported anxiety before your period in three logged cycles. This pattern does not establish its cause.”** Show supporting dates, eligible-cycle denominator and missingness. [Schmalenberger et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12905-024-03273-y)

**4a: hourly soaking plus dizziness.** “Doctor today” and “A&E or clinic” understate urgency. KKH directs heavy vaginal bleeding to immediate assessment; SCDF lists excessive bleeding among emergencies. Proposed wording: **“Heavy bleeding with dizziness needs urgent assessment now. Go to an emergency department. Call 995 for fainting, breathlessness or excessive/uncontrolled bleeding.”** A numeric duration threshold must not delay care when someone is unwell. [KKH](https://www.kkh.com.sg/our-specialties/urgent-og-centre), [SCDF](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/home/about-scdf/emergency-medical-services)

**4a: “This advice was written with clinicians”.** Require named reviewers, credentials, exact reviewed content, version/date and approval records. Collaboration does not substantiate every generated reply. Until documented: **“This alert uses published guidance. It cannot assess your condition.”**

**4c: “Only you” and “encrypted backup only you can unlock”.** These promises do not describe current Ovumind. Backup JSON is unencrypted (`journal.html:198`); encrypted clinician sharing is separate. Sync remains unwired (`supabase/migrations/20261018110000_sync.sql:2`). Exclusive-key backup requires a reviewed key, recovery and threat model.

Honest wording: **“Records are stored in this browser. When online AI is enabled, notes or summary inputs are sent through Supabase to the model provider.”** Current default-on AI and automatic journal insights contradict “nothing is uploaded” (`ovu-orb.js:75/:83`; `journal.html:205`; `room.js:1185`). Regex redaction is not demonstrated de-identification. Camera non-upload claims need network verification; no-sale claims need SDK, policy and contract evidence.

Also replace 1b’s “charting helps a lot” with “may help you notice patterns”. In 2a, “this morning” does not justify “about 7:00”. In 1a, more logs may improve estimates, but precision is not guaranteed.

**Naming recommendation**

Keep **Ovumind** as the app, **Luna** as its digital companion and **Erin Lee** as the identifiable human coach. Use **ERIN** only as a clearly labelled programme name. Adding Ebb would create another identity without functional benefit. The competing masthead and “mindfully aged” tagline (`room.html:98–102`) blur the hierarchy and narrow the emotional framing. Prefer “Her body, her rhythm, mindfully.”
