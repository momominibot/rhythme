# App Store frames, 21 October 2026

Eight frames at 1320 x 2868 (iPhone 6.9 inch portrait), one continuous sky from the first to the last (each frame shows
the next window of one wide painted sky, so the set reads as a single picture when swiped). Jamie, 21 Oct: "for the app
logo i want the avatar palm up showing the floating rhythme logo, build the insights of the app to promote in the app
store at the same time. i want the frame to be continuous from first to end picture."

| Frame | What it shows | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 01 hero | her, palm up, the floating Rhythme emblem (Seedream 5.0 pro, two references: her base sprite and the app icon; cut out with rembg) | Rhythme. Your cycle, your room, your pace |
| 02 room | the home room, Peach the corgi, Today's check-in | A room of your own |
| 03 sleep | the bedtime sheet | Sleep, noticed gently |
| 04 erin | Erin's morning, part 1 of 3 | Erin's morning, in her voice |
| 05 world | the harbour bench, Stop watching | A world to breathe in |
| 06 spring | the hidden spring with the tone playing | A hidden spring |
| 07 clinician | doctor.html with the fictional sample | Notes for your clinician |
| 08 yours | the You tab, cloud backup off | Your diary stays yours |

Every caption is a plain description of something in the frame; none promises an outcome. Screenshots were taken from
this build with `shoot.py` (390 x 844 at 3x, reduced motion, seeded diary, the account name "Smoke" is the smoke
fixture's) and composed with `compose.py` (Newsreader for titles, Manrope for the lines, both from Google Fonts). The
JPEGs here are for review; the PNG originals (about 3 MB each) are what goes to App Store Connect and were sent to Jamie
in the session.
