# The pets walk (19 October 2026)

Jamie, after the camera shipped: "The pets will also have to learn how to walk normally." Today the six pets sit where
they were placed and breathe (a slow scale bob), hop when fed and wiggle when played with; they never cross the floor.
This page is the research pass and the plan.

## What the sources say

1. **The quadruped walk is two offset human walks.** Game Developer's guide to four-legged animation builds the walk
   from four major poses (two stride, two passing), treats the hips and chest of each side as offset bouncing balls and
   describes the whole thing as "two offset human walk cycles", with the head animated as a third bouncing ball for
   follow-through and the tail last. Citing Muybridge, it says most quadrupeds share one footfall pattern per gait,
   elephants and kangaroos excepted; it shows an eight-phase horse walk as reference
   ([Animating Four-Legged Beasts](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/animating-four-legged-beasts)).
2. **Cats walk in lateral sequence.** A figure drawn from Muybridge's plates labels the cat's walk RH-RF-LH-LF: right
   hind, right fore, left hind, left fore, each foreleg landing after the hind leg on its own side ("the nonprimate is
   walking in lateral sequence"), unlike a primate's diagonal sequence
   ([PLoS ONE figure, Muybridge reference](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4100729/figure/pone-0101758-g001)).
3. **Pixel-art practice.** The Pixelblog on motion cycles keeps eight frames for consistency across its studies, notes
   that stylised game sprites can use fewer, and for the dog walk says "the back leg follows one step behind the front
   leg of the opposite side" and that "the distance traveled is the same for both the front and back legs" so the body
   does not stretch; shoulders and pelvis "undulate with opposite but equal motion"
   ([Pixelblog 25, Motion Cycles](https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2020/1/23/pixelblog-25-motion-cycles)).
4. **The planner.** jevauto picked a sprite-sheet engine with distance-driven playback (the approach here) and
   suggested a canvas and Perlin-noise wandering; the scene is DOM and the pets must route around furniture, so the
   wander uses the walk engine's A* on the same floor mask instead, and the canvas is not needed.

## The plan for Rhythme

- **Sheets from the model, per pet and direction.** `tools/art/gen.py` with each pet's sitting sprite composited on
  chroma green as the reference: "exactly 8 frames in a single horizontal row, all four paws on the same ground line,
  SIDE VIEW facing RIGHT / walking AWAY seen from BEHIND / walking TOWARDS the viewer, legs alternating like a real
  kitten (puppy) walk, head and tail gently bobbing", 2048x1024, one call per sheet, no retries, the batch stops on the
  first provider failure. Six pets (Mochi, Peach, Mewmew, Sesame, Mango, Hana) by three directions, eighteen sheets.
- **Slicing** (`tools/art/walkcycle.py`, extended): frames are runs of non-green columns as before; for pets the sheet
  is scaled so the median frame's head width matches the sitting sprite's head width (the head is the one feature a
  chibi keeps between sitting and walking; heights do not transfer, a walking cat is lower and longer), each frame sits
  on the cell's bottom edge centred on its own box, and the sidecar JSON records, in cell pixels, a head anchor per
  frame and the sitting sprite's head anchor, so a worn bow or hat can ride along.
- **Playback** (`ovu-petwalk.js`, new): a strip box inside the pet's body shows one frame by translating the strip;
  the sitting sprite is hidden while the pet walks and shown again when it stops; frames step by distance (eight per
  stride, a stride of speed / 8 fps x 8 frames iso units, so cadence follows speed); the strip is chosen from the
  movement direction (side, mirrored for left; away; towards) with the same rule as hers. A missing strip falls back
  to a small step bob. A worn piece (bow, hat, bandana, charm) is drawn inside the strip box at the frame's head
  anchor, offset as it is from the sitting head, so it flips and scales with the frame.
- **Wandering.** Each placed pet rests five to sixteen seconds, then picks a free floor point within reach (at most
  22 iso units from where it stands and 34 from its home spot) that the walk engine can plan a route to around the
  furniture, the other props and her feet; walks there at a cat's 11 or a dog's 13 iso units a second with an ease-out
  at the end; rests again. Every fourth trip goes home, so pets drift but never wander off. Pets wander in the home
  layouts and the garden (the only scenes that show them). Nothing moves while a sheet is open, during a session or
  practice, while arranging, at night, or while she sleeps; a tap on a walking pet stops it where it is and opens its
  care bubble as before. The live position survives the props re-render (sky changes, feeding) and resets on a layout
  change. Reduced motion: the pets stay put.
- **Depth and collisions.** Pets already count as solids and as sprites in the depth sort; the walk engine re-reads
  their positions every frame, so a pet that has moved is routed around on her next tap, and the depth order is
  re-ranked after each pet step. The arranged positions are untouched: arranging keeps using the saved spot.
- **Untouched.** `ovu-rewards.js` (the catalogue is read only), `ovu-pets.js` (moods, feeding, wear spots), the
  room's pet care sheet and poop buttons, outfits, night and sky rules, `#welcome` and `#consent`, podcasts, Listen.

## Tests

- `tools/test-core.js`: stride and frame maths for cats and dogs; a trip target is always on the floor, off the
  solids, within reach and leash, and comes with a route; in every home layout and the garden each pet's home spot can
  start a trip (so no pet is boxed in); the worn-piece mapping keeps a bow at the same offset from the head when the
  head is scaled; the fallback when a sheet is missing.
- `tools/e2e_smoke.py`: nudging the cat in the room starts a walk (the strip shows, frames change, the sitting sprite
  hides), the position changes and lands on the floor, the sitting sprite returns; a tap on the walking pet stops it
  and opens its care bubble; pets stay put while the bedtime sheet is open; no console errors.
