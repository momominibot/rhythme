## The loop in one line

Log your day and earn hearts, buy seeds at the grocer on Dream Street, plant with an intention, water each day as
one mindful minute, watch the bed grow over real days, harvest with thanks, then cook, brew, share or feed your pet.
Cooking ends with "Did you eat something like this today?", which opens the food diary only if she says yes.

## 1. The grocer

- A fourth store on Dream Street, with its own storefront on the wide street and its own interior plate in the
  same Seedream style. Open 8 am to 8 pm, like the other stores keep their own hours. Name to choose (section 9).
- A shopkeeper neighbour who says one short line on each visit (the plan's NPCs are mindfulness teachers).
- Shelves:
  - **Seed packets.** One packet plants one bed. 3 to 8 hearts (table in section 2).
  - **Kitchen staples** for recipes: rice, eggs, noodles, tofu, garlic, coconut milk, honey, yoghurt. 2 to 4 hearts.
  - Later: a bigger watering can that waters two beds a trip.
- **Seed swap counter.** Bring 3 of any harvest, take one packet of any crop she has grown before. No hearts change
  hands, so a quiet logging week never stops the farm, and harvests never turn into hearts.
- The shed's three free seeds a day go away. The first visit to the grocer gives one radish and one bok choy packet,
  so a new player can start the same day.

## 3. The growing process

1. **Soil.** An empty bed. She stands at its nearest side (the stand points are built).
2. **Planting.** Choose a packet from her bag. Cost 2 energy. Cue: "Press the seed in. Set one small intention for
   this week." The bed shows a tiny marker with the crop's picture.
3. **Watering.** One mindful minute (built for the garden plot, with Erin's voice). Cost 6 energy. Dark soil shows it
   is watered today.
4. **Sprout, young, flowers.** Each stage has its own picture per crop. A weedy morning pauses that bed until the
   weeds are pulled (built). Cost 5 energy. Cue: "Notice what you are letting go of."
5. **Ripe.** The bed sparkles. It stays ripe for 3 real days.
6. **Gone to seed.** After 3 ripe days the bed is not lost: it drops one packet of the same crop into her bag and
   clears. No rotting, no guilt, and a reason to come back.
7. **Regrowing crops** return to the young stage after a harvest and ripen again on the cadence in the table.

Seasons: the HUD already names a season. In this version every crop grows all year; a season list per crop can come
later. Rain is a later idea (a rainy real day waters every bed).

## 4. The final process: harvest

- A tap on a ripe bed: she kneels for about ten seconds while one line shows: "Before you pick, notice the colour,
  the smell, the weight in your hand. Thank the plant." Then the harvest pops into the basket with a sparkle.
- Cost 4 energy. No hearts: hearts stay with logging.
- The first harvest of each crop is kept in a small "garden journal" with its date, so the farm has a collection to
  complete without a reward loop.

## 5. What the harvest is for

1. **Cook at home.** A kitchen counter hotspot in each home. A recipe needs harvest plus staples. Cooking is a one to
   three minute guided practice in Erin's voice, like the everyday practices: wash, chop, smell, taste. It ends with
   the dish on the table and "Did you eat something like this today?". Yes opens the food diary filled in; nothing is
   saved unless she saves it, and that entry records `source: 'game_prompt_confirmed'`. The practice minutes count
   as mindfulness under the current flat rule. First recipes:
   - Garlic bok choy (bok choy, garlic)
   - Kang kong stir-fry (kang kong, garlic)
   - Tomato and egg (tomato, eggs, rice)
   - Pumpkin soup (pumpkin, coconut milk)
   - Carrot and radish salad (carrot, radish, honey)
   - Sweet potato ginger soup (sweet potato, ginger)
   - Strawberry yoghurt bowl (strawberry, yoghurt, honey)
   - Pandan rice (pandan, rice)
2. **Brew in the greenhouse.** Herbs become a cup of tea with a one-minute tea practice: warmth in the hands, the
   steam, the first sip. Each tea links to its research card in the greenhouse (built today). No health claims in the
   game itself.
3. **Share.** Give produce to a neighbour on Dream Street or the plaza. It raises that neighbour's friendship, a
   separate meter from the hearts wallet. Cue: "Wish them well as you hand it over." Sometimes a neighbour gives a
   recipe card or a seed back.
4. **Feed your pet.** Carrot, pumpkin and sweet potato are pet treats (plain pumpkin for cats too). They fill the
   pet's "full" meter under the existing pet care rules. Garlic and onion never go to a pet.
5. **Swap for seeds** at the grocer: 3 of any harvest for one packet.
6. **Decorate.** A bowl of fruit or a vase of chamomile in the room.

Selling produce for hearts is left out on purpose, so hearts keep meaning "I logged my day".

## 6. Mindfulness in every activity

| Activity | Cue | Word |
|---|---|---|
| Buying seeds | Choose one thing you would like to grow in yourself this week | cetanā, intention |
| Planting | Press the seed in and set that intention | cetanā |
| Watering | One ordinary minute, fully lived (built) | sati, mindfulness |
| Pulling weeds | Notice what you are letting go of | vossagga, letting go |
| Waiting for growth | Growth keeps its own pace | khanti, patience |
| Harvest | Colour, smell, weight; thank the plant | kataññutā, gratitude |
| Cooking | Wash, chop, smell, taste, one step at a time | sati |
| Tea | Warmth, steam, the first sip | sati |
| Sharing | Wish them well as you hand it over | mettā, kindness |
| Walking a pet | Walk at her pace and notice what catches her eye (built) | sati |

Lines are written first and voiced by Erin later through the existing voice pipeline.

## 7. Data rules (unchanged from the game plan)

- A game event never writes a wellness entry. "Log a meal?" opens the diary; only her save writes, with its source.
- Hearts never come from farming, cooking, sharing or swapping.
- When inventory and hearts sync to the account, every purchase, swap, harvest and gift carries an operation id, as
  the plan's data section requires.

## 8. Art and build order

packet; the swap takes 3 and gives 1; no farm action changes hearts; buy, plant, water over faked dates, harvest, cook
and see "Log a meal?" open the diary in the browser.
