# Seats: every bench its own practice, its own weather

Jamie, 21 Oct 2026: "each of the sitting interface should be different with some animation and different practice with
erin, i want it refreshing for each chair... for the sitting in the cave will be different - it's purely frequency
sounds".

Built on 21 Oct 2026 (branch `claude/seats`, on top of `claude/world-harbour`). The shape follows a Codex design review
of the same morning (gpt-6-astra, read-only, with the code and web search): **a seated mode of its own rather than a
sprite swap inside `runPractice`; the same controls as Morning start (Pause, Next, Voice, Stop); a minute of active time
before anything is saved; one thank-you a day per physical seat; the cave as the one seat with no plain sit; effects that
are environmental and never timed to breath.** Its script rewrites were taken whole (optional phrasing in the kindness
practice before any distress, no straining to hear, a reduced-motion line for the water, a plain close).

## How a seated practice runs (`ovu-seats.js`)

- **The sheet** names the practice, its length and what it is for, offers Begin and the plain sit, and says in one line
  what is saved and what is not: a practice of a minute or more can be saved, with the usual small thank-you once a day
  for this seat; sitting is just sitting. The credit reads exactly: *Rhythme's words, read in Erin Lee's cloned voice
  with her permission.*
- **Begin** claims the room the way Morning start does: `emit('activity')` first (the care steps, a float on the water
  and the greenhouse minute step aside), the walk engine and the room's own walk stop, the idle walk home is cancelled,
  the podcast pauses. Her seated sprite is mounted on the bench through the garden's `seatPlace`/`seatedImg`; the
  standing avatar is hidden outright (not only faded), so the camera does not follow an invisible figure. `OvuRoom.busy()`
  is true for the whole practice: a floor tap goes nowhere, a hotspot tap says "Stop to get up first." and the bed still
  opens.
- **The panel** is the Morning start panel's shape with its own ids (`#spanel`): step n of m, the caption in a polite live
  region, Pause/Resume, Next (Finish on the last line), Voice off/on, Stop. One audio element, created and first played
  inside the tap, carries every line. A step's time is its clip plus Erin's pause plus 0.6 s; a line still being said when
  its time is up is waited for, a second at a time, at most eight.
- **The clock** counts only while a step is running: never while paused, never while the page is hidden (hiding the page
  pauses the practice and the voice; she resumes by hand).
- **The end**: she stays seated as on any bench (the plain sit, tap to get up), and only then is she asked. Under sixty
  unrounded seconds of active time nothing is recorded ("Stopped. Nothing under a minute is recorded."). From a minute:
  "Save this minute / these n minutes to your journal?" with Save practice and Not this time; a failed save keeps the
  sheet for a retry; another open sheet (a symptoms form, the bed) is never replaced, the question waits until it closes.
- **The entry**: `kind: 'informal'`, `activity: 'seat'`, the physical `seat`, the scene, the practice name, seconds and
  minutes, `finished`/`stoppedEarly`, `confirmedReal`, `startedAt` and the credit. Hearts: the flat practice points inside
  the day's cap, and nothing for the same bench again today, however the entry got there (deleted and redone, imported,
  a clock set back): `OvuRewards` keeps a per-day map of seats that have said thank you.
- **Cancel** (sleep, clear, import, leaving the scene, another activity, bedtime's `stopEverything`): the practice ends at
  once with nothing asked and nothing left behind: no sitter, no panel, no effects, no pending question.

## The cave (`ovu-tones.js`)

Sitting by the water is the tone. The warm stone and the crystals open the same sheet and walk her to the same stand
point. Sit by the water starts the sound first and seats her only if the phone could make it; one button on the scene,
"Stop sound and stand up", ends both, and so do the chosen time running out, leaving the spring, a walk, sleep, a hidden
page, clear or import. The sheet says what the tones are and are not (docs/design/2026-10-21-cave-tones.md). Nothing is
written, paid or spent.

## The effects (`#seatfx`)

One pooled layer inside the plate's inner box (so the camera carries it), below the sitter, `pointer-events: none`,
`aria-hidden`. Each element has its own place, size, duration and a negative delay, so nothing moves in step with
anything else and nothing can be read as a breathing pacer: petals and leaves fall on 9 to 16 second paths, motes rise on
6 to 11, ripples open from offset origins on 5.5 to 9.5, light streaks slide on 5 to 8, crystal glints fade on 7 and 11
seconds (opacity only, no scaling). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every animation stops: a few still petals and leaves at
low opacity, no motes, no rings. The layer follows the plain sits too (the garden, the street, the plaza, the harbour,
the cave), so a bench feels like itself whether or not she takes the practice.
