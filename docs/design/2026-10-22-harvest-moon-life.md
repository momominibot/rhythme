## What Harvest Moon does (sources checked 22 Oct)

- Every animal has an affection meter. Daily care (talking, brushing, feeding) raises it, and higher affection gives
  better products: a new hen lays small eggs that improve with affection; sheep give more wool.
  ([fandom, BTN](https://harvestmoon.fandom.com/wiki/Harvest_Sprites_(BTN)), [BINUS retrospective](https://socs.binus.ac.id/?p=22742))
- The coop grows from five chickens to ten through a carpenter upgrade. ([BINUS](https://socs.binus.ac.id/?p=22742))
- Villagers have a heart meter from black (acquaintance) to red (love). Only the first gift each day counts, liked
  gifts raise it most. A proposal needs a red heart, every heart event seen, an enlarged house and the Blue Feather.
  ([fandom, Blue Feather](https://harvestmoon.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Feather_(BTN)), [Fogu, marriage](https://fogu.com/hm14/marriage/index.html))

## Rhythme's rules that stay

- Hearts (the currency) come from logging; play earns none, practice a little (checkpoints 22, 33). Buying animals,
  barn upgrades and the feather costs hearts, like the dream house.
- The game never writes a health entry (checkpoint 29). Cooking may still offer the food diary, as today.
- No guilt: animals never get sick or leave, love never goes down, villagers never get upset with her.
- Energy, not punishment (checkpoint 30).
- Villagers are original characters. Real people's names or faces (for example the actors Zhang Linghe and Jing Boran)
  are not used: Rhythme cannot use a real person's name or likeness without their permission. Original characters can
  share the warm C-drama feel Jamie wants.

## Phases

1. **Animal love** (PR momominibot/rhythme#68, built): a love meter of 0 to 10 per animal; pet, brush and treat add one
   a day each; hearts pop with the pet sound; hens wander and peck.
2. **Products and the bag**: milk and egg size follow love (0 to 3 small, 4 to 7 medium, 8 to 10 large, as in Harvest
   Moon). Her bag (You > Bag) lists milk, eggs, crops and dishes. Cooked dishes go into the bag as well as offering
   the diary.
3. **The ranch** (new land past the farm, a new plate): a ranch shop to buy a cow or a hen with hearts; barn and coop
   upgrades for more stalls (barn 1 to 4 cows, coop 2 to 6 hens); babies: a hen's egg in the incubator hatches a chick
   in 3 days and grows into a hen in 7; a cow at 10 hearts for 7 days has a calf that grows in 14.
4. **Villagers**: four original villagers living in the town (two she can court, two friends). Each has liked and
   loved gifts from the bag (dishes count most). Talk once a day and one gift a day raise their hearts, shown black to
   red. Heart events at 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 are short illustrated scenes. At a red heart, with every event seen and the
   dream house, she can buy a blue feather at the ranch shop and propose; the wedding is a festival scene.
5. **Festivals**: one small festival a week on the plaza (flower, cooking contest, star night).
