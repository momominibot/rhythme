## The Starlight gown (the first wardrobe drop)

- One look: a floor-length lilac-to-blush ombré satin gown under sheer tulle, a bodice of tiny silver star sequins, a slim silver belt, silver heels; her own face, bun and star clip. Rendered in one guarded Seedream run (`scratchpad/gown/stand.sh`, then `batch.sh`: a lock, a `RUN_STARTED` marker, finished files skipped, no retries, stop on the first failure or any stderr): the standing sprite from the base avatar, then the nineteen action poses, meditate, sit and sleeping as two-reference edits (image 1 the pose, image 2 the gown), 23 renders of 30 to 40 seconds each, no failure.
- Fitting: every copy is keyed with the largest shape and fitted onto its base pose's canvas by figure height (`fit_all.py`). A skirt wider than the base canvas gets a **wider canvas, never a shorter figure**; those sprites carry their own `AVATAR_FIT` entry (width factor against the 318 px standing canvas, head-centre fraction measured on the head band and offset by 0.018 to match the hand-set table). The strip rule in `fitAvatar` knows the `-gown` suffix, so unwidened copies share their pose's numbers.
- In the wardrobe the gown is a **Rhythme Plus drop**: listed with a lock and the words With Rhythme Plus until Plus is on this phone (a preview code from You, or the account; the same `OvuPodcast.plusActive()` the podcast uses) or tester mode. Tapping the locked gown keeps her clothes and opens Rhythme Plus. `outfitLocked` also guards `setOutfit`, so a profile that still says gown after Plus lapses dresses her in the knit. The plaza look list (`ovu-friends.js` LOOKS) carries `gown`, and the server's look check (`^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{1,29}$`) accepts it.
- No walk strips yet: like the kebayas and the tudung, the gown falls back to the step bob while she walks. Three strips (side, away, towards) are one more guarded run when Jamie wants them.

## Her birthday

Jamie, later on 21 Oct: "I just want a birthing wishing with a good life advice direction based on the birthdate entered during onboarding." Then, on the year: "many other apps can ask for birthdate".

- **Where she gives it**: the intro (Getting to know you) has an optional step just before her name, "Your birthday (optional)": a month, a day and an optional year. The same three selects sit in You. Stored as `profile.birthday = 'MM-DD'` and `profile.birthYear` (a number, or null). Skip clears both.
- **The year is for adults only**: the list starts 18 years back, and a year that would make her under 18 today (or a 29 February in a year without one) is refused with a kind line and the step waits. Rhythme's notice says it closes an account it learns belongs to someone under 18, so it never stores such a year.
- **The promises, rewritten**: the notice (privacy.html), the terms and the consent sheet in the room used to say Rhythme never asks a date of birth. They now say an optional age range and an optional birthday may be asked, with the year only if she adds it, kept on the phone and used only for the card; the 18+ gate still keeps only her confirmation. The intro's age-range step no longer says it never asks a date of birth.
- **Where it is kept**: on this phone only. `ovu-sync.js` leaves `birthday` and `birthYear` out of the cloud copy in both directions (`DEVICE_KEYS`), and no health screen, note, insight or relay reads them. A copy of her data that she exports herself includes them, and the notice says so. The older pages' copy of the intro (`ovuluna_onboarding`) never holds them; copies written before this build are cleaned at boot.
- **Notice version 5** (21 October 2026): the consent sheet promises to ask again when what Rhythme collects changes, so the notice moved from 4 to 5. Everyone who agreed to version 4 sees the sheet once more, opening with "New in this notice: Rhythme can ask for your birthday...". The notice hash now covers the sheet's birthday line (`NOTICE_LINES` in ovu-consent.js). The server accepts any version, so no migration is needed.
- **The day**: the room greets her, a cake with balloons stands on her table for seven days, and the card opens once (straight away when she gives today's date). Tapping the cake reopens it.
- **The card**: her name and the date, **one warm wish**; with the year, **who she is from the day she was born**, her BaZi Day Master in the names and words of Jamie's YourDigitalDNA (for example "The Field: nurturing and resourceful... You give until it costs, because you can grow anything. The work is choosing whose soil you are."), computed with the standard day count, which equals YourDigitalDNA's engine with the hour unknown on 300 test dates and two published anchors; and **a direction for the year ahead** from the Pythagorean personal year (month, day and the year the birthday falls in, reduced to 1 to 9), so it changes every birthday, each with a first step offered as an invitation. Then Wear the party outfit (the gown when Plus is on) and Thank you. No hearts.
- **Honesty on the card**: one line says how both are read, that others born on the same day share them, and that it is a reflection, not a prediction or health advice.
- **What came from YourDigitalDNA**: the ten Day Master names, lines and reframes, with Rhythme's punctuation. Its engine, its other readings and its reference charts stay in its private repository.

## Codex verification fixes (21 Oct, evening)

- **Help improve logging on a renewed notice**: a choice the account already holds is not sent again. The account's consent function sets `accepted_at` to now on every grant, and the staff metrics read entries from that time, so a re-grant would have dropped entries the consent already covered. The improvement words and their hash are unchanged under version 5. A tick kept only on this phone (no account yet) still goes through `setImprove`, so the first sign-in can record it.
- **"Anything happening right now?"**: both intro buttons start from her answers as they are now, except this one, which is about today and is always asked afresh.
- **The card's timing**: it opens by itself only after the room has stayed free for half a second, so Window's Bedtime (close the sheet, then start bedtime) is never taken for a free room. If another sheet replaces it within 1.5 seconds and she has not touched it, it does not count as shown and opens again once the room is free. Closing it herself, or touching it, counts.
- **A stored year outside the list** (for example 1899, or one that would make her under 18) is shown as it is, marked as not used, so she can see it and change or remove it.
