Language and market coverage: 21 English-language Western experts, 6 Chinese-language or Chinese-population teams (Liu, Mak, Wong, Tang, Sun, Tian), 1 Singapore team (Lim/Brahm Centre), 4 Islamic or Malay sources (Isgandarova, Ahmad review, Saged, MUIS). We found no published mindfulness trial in Singapore women in any life stage, and none in Malay Muslim women in Singapore.

## Agreement map

- **Agree:** mindfulness gives small benefits over doing nothing and is about as good as other active approaches (Goldberg, Gál, Linardon, Mak, Wong). Acceptance, not attention alone, carries the stress benefit (Creswell; consistent with Neff's self-compassion work). A minority feel worse, and trauma raises the risk (Britton, Goldberg, Farias).
- **Disagree or mixed:** brief and micro practice (Schumer: g 0.21, about 0.04 after publication bias; JITAI g 0.15) versus Tang's short training effects. Everyday informal practice (Birtwell, Kakoschke: linked to wellbeing) versus Canby (did not predict improvement; teacher and group did). Perinatal benefit: older RCT pooling found none (Dhillon 2017); newer, mostly Chinese, trials find moderate benefits (Liu 2025, Sun 2021, Tian 2025); a 2-week app for parents found none (Ratcliff 2025).
- **Correction:** DeepSeek's suggestion of paced breathing for hot flushes is contradicted by two RCTs (Carpenter 2013, Huang 2015); the guideline-backed approach is menopause-specific CBT (NICE NG23).
- **False corroboration check:** the app meta-analyses (Gál 2021, Linardon 2020/2024) share many of the same Headspace and Calm trials, so they are not independent confirmations. Liu 2025 pools Chinese-database trials that likely overlap Sun 2021. Wen 2024's infertility SMDs of -2.25 are implausibly large and point to weak trials.
- **Model claims excluded after checking:** Doubao named nine Singapore, Malaysian, Indonesian and Bruneian experts and statistics (an 18% religious-distress figure from a "2021 KKH trial", an "IMH 2023 ban", a "2022 SGH study"); none could be found on PubMed or the web, so none are used. DeepSeek's Peking University, CUHK and Tang Yi-Yuan leads were verified and used.
