**Gate D: revise the evidence base and topic list before scripting.** The main problems are overgeneralisation, mismatched populations and unsupported app claims. This was read-only; no files changed. Undated sources below were accessed on 20 October 2026.

1. **Must change | E6–7, E22–23; A3, C3, F1: separate menstrual and postpartum bleeding thresholds.**  
   [CDC, 15 May 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/): “soaking through one or more pads in an hour.” [KKH UOGC, undated](https://www.kkh.com.sg/our-specialties/urgent-og-centre): “significant abdominal pain or heavy vaginal bleeding” requires immediate attention.  
   Recommended wording: **“After birth, sudden heavy bleeding, soaking a pad in an hour, or passing clots larger than an egg needs urgent assessment now. With any bleeding, severe pain, faintness or breathlessness means get emergency help.”** Do not require several hours of postpartum bleeding. Menstrual coin-sized clots are an assessment cue, not a universal emergency threshold.

2. **Must change | E20; A6, C1, F1: pregnancy testing must not delay emergency care.**  
   [KKH ectopic pregnancy guidance, undated](https://www.kkh.com.sg/symptoms-treatments/ectopic-pregnancy) lists “Pain in your shoulder tip” and “Feeling faint or dizzy.”  
   Use: **“If pregnancy is possible and you have severe abdominal pain, shoulder-tip pain, dizziness or fainting, get emergency help now. Do not wait for a home test or app result.”** Ectopic bleeding can be internal.

3. **Must change | E22–23; C3: six weeks is not the safety boundary.**  
   [CDC, 15 May 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/): “during pregnancy and in the year after delivery.” Include fever **38°C or higher**, severe persistent headache or vision changes, chest pain and difficulty breathing as separate reasons for immediate assessment. Lower temperature does not negate other serious symptoms. Rename C3 to include recovery **and warning signs beyond six weeks**; advise telling clinicians about a pregnancy within the past year.

4. **Must change | E24–26; C4, F1: distinguish intrusive thoughts from psychosis or intent.**  
   [RCPsych perinatal OCD, November 2021](https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/mental-health/mental-illnesses-and-mental-health-problems/perinatal-ocd): “people with OCD don't become violent or act on these thoughts.” [Postpartum psychosis, November 2018](https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/mental-health/mental-illnesses-and-mental-health-problems/postpartum-psychosis): “a psychiatric emergency.”  
   Frightening unwanted thoughts warrant supportive clinical assessment. Hallucinations, delusions, marked confusion, intent to harm or inability to remain safe require emergency help. Do not diagnose OCD through a video or tell someone with severe symptoms to wait two weeks.

5. **Must change | E34–35; D5: checked promptly does not always mean ambulance.**  
   [NHS, reviewed 24 September 2026](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/post-menopausal-bleeding/): “even if it’s only a small amount or it's only happened once.”  
   Say: **“Arrange a medical appointment for any bleeding after menopause, even one spot. Heavy bleeding, severe pain or faintness needs emergency assessment.”** Do not transplant NICE’s age-specific cancer-referral pathway or UK appointment guarantees into Singapore.

6. **Must change | E12–16; B1, F2: remove “pinpoint your unique window.”**  
   [ASRM, 2022 committee opinion](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/optimizing-natural-fertility-a-committee-opinion-2021/): “The timing of the fertile window within a given cycle can vary considerably.”  
   The approximate six-day biological window does not validate Rhythme’s predictions. Explain uncertainty, including irregular cycles. Cervical mucus is useful information, not confirmation of ovulation. No app-specific validation was supplied for contraception, ovulation detection or conception probability.

7. **Must change | E28–29; D3–4: “MHT is safe” is inadequate.**  
   The [full Singapore guideline, launched 20 February 2026](https://www.for.sg/menopause-guidelines), says benefits “typically outweigh the potential risks” for most appropriately selected symptomatic women under 60 or within ten years of menopause.  
   Specify individual assessment, treatment type and contraindications. Distinguish systemic MHT from vaginal oestrogen; systemic oestrogen generally needs endometrial protection when the uterus is present. Do not promote MHT for routine cardiovascular or dementia prevention in asymptomatic women reaching menopause at the usual age.

8. **Must change | D3: FDA label changes are not a declaration of risk-free treatment.**  
   [FDA, 12 February 2026](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-labeling-changes-menopausal-hormone-therapy-products): “six menopausal hormone therapy products.” Its [10 November 2025 request](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-requests-labeling-changes-related-safety-information-clarify-benefitrisk-considerations) says “Retain information about cardiovascular diseases and breast cancer warnings.”  
   Describe the specific label changes accurately. They neither remove all risks nor automatically change Singapore prescribing rules.

9. **Must change | E2–4; A5: the PMOS rename is real, but adoption is transitional.**  
   [Monash, 13 May 2026](https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/polyendocrine-metabolic-ovarian-syndrome-new-name-to-improve-diagnosis-and-care-of-condition-affecting-170-million-women-worldwide): “three year transition period,” with implementation planned through the 2028 guideline update.  
   Use **“PCOS, now also called PMOS”** so viewers recognise existing diagnoses and clinic terminology. Do not imply universal adoption or changed diagnostic criteria merely because the name changed.

10. **Must change | E1–2; A1: preserve the purpose and population of cycle limits.**  
    [FIGO review, 4 August 2023](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijgo.14946): “provided there has been no pregnancy or puerperium.” It supports 24–38 days and bleeding up to eight days, with age-specific regularity limits.  
    These are descriptive reproductive-age parameters, not reassurance despite symptoms. PCOS assessment thresholds answer a different question. Do not apply one range indiscriminately postpartum or throughout perimenopause, or require six months of logging before seeking help.

11. **Must change | E8–11; A4: hormonal treatment is not a compulsory gate before diagnosis.**  
    [ESHRE, 2022](https://academic.oup.com/hropen/article/2022/2/hoac009/6537540): “no evidence of superiority of either approach,” comparing diagnostic laparoscopy with imaging plus empirical treatment. Preserve shared decisions and situations where empirical treatment is inappropriate. Normal imaging does not exclude endometriosis.  
    Add [ACOG’s new diagnostic guideline, 1 March 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41712950/), covering “clinical, imaging, and surgical evaluation.” Its full recommendations were not independently accessible here.

12. **Should change | E9: distinguish emerging tests from adopted guidance.**  
    [NICE, 14 September 2026](https://nice-newsroom.prgloo.com/news/embargoed-nice-updates-draft-guidance-on-diagnosing-endometriosis-in-primary-care), describes “updated its draft guidance” on a saliva test during evidence generation. This is not routine Singapore screening or permission to claim a definitive saliva diagnosis. I located no newer complete ESHRE endometriosis guideline replacing 2022.

13. **Must change | E30–41; D2, E4: correct both positive and negative mindfulness overclaims.**  
    [NAMS, June 2023](https://menopause.org/wp-content/uploads/professional/2023-nonhormone-therapy-position-statement.pdf): “not enough data to recommend MBSR for the management of VMS.”  
    Say **insufficient evidence to recommend mindfulness for hot-flush treatment**, not “proved ineffective.” Mood, sleep, hot-flush frequency and symptom bother are different outcomes. An educational clip or minute-long app practice cannot inherit results from structured courses. “No established fertility benefit” is preferable to an absolute claim of no effect.

14. **Must change | E41, local ledger L57: correct the intervention dose and null-result interpretation.**  
    The [Singapore pilot, 19 August 2025](https://mental.jmir.org/2025/1/e77793), used one ten-minute session daily for three days, not three daily sessions, and “was not powered to detect small effects.”  
    Its null comparison does not establish equivalence or universal failure of self-guided apps. Remove guaranteed “minutes of calm” and lasting-benefit claims. Retain an easy stop option; [September 2026 research](https://mental.jmir.org/2026/1/e95628) found participants “no more likely to report a distressing experience” than ordinary internet-use controls, so do not attribute every reported distress event to meditation.

15. **Must change | E14–15; B3: use Singapore fertility advice.**  
    [MOH, 5 February 2024](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/tracking-number-of-women-and-men-who-have-undergone-fertility-screening-at-public-and-private-hospitals/) specifies one year below 35 and six months above 35; [KKH, undated](https://www.kkh.com.sg/our-specialties/kkivf-centre/2-seek-help), explicitly flags “Women aged 35 years and above.”  
    Recommended synthesis: **one year under 35, six months from 35, earlier with irregular/absent cycles or known concerns**. Do not import NICE’s age-36-at-presentation rule as Singapore policy.

16. **Should change | E17; B2: localise folic acid.**  
    [KKH, undated](https://www.kkh.com.sg/our-specialties/kkivf-centre/2-improve-fertility): “400mcg (0.4mg) per day prior to conception up to 12 weeks gestation.”  
    Use **400 micrograms daily before conception through week 12**, with clinician-prescribed higher doses for selected circumstances. Avoid suggesting everyone needs 800 micrograms or 5 mg.

17. **Should change | E43; A6: separate screening from symptoms.**  
    [HPB Healthier SG screening, undated](https://www.healthhub.sg/programmes/healthiersg-screening/screening-faq): “Pap test for those aged 25 to 29 years old.” The programme uses three-year Pap and five-year HPV intervals from 30 for eligible previously sexually active women.  
    Give screening its own topic. Bleeding after sex or between periods needs assessment irrespective of screening eligibility or a recent normal result.

18. **Should change | C3, C5: add follow-up and contraception planning.**  
    [WHO, 30 March 2022](https://www.who.int/news/item/30-03-2022-who-urges-quality-care-for-women-and-newborns-in-critical-first-weeks-after-childbirth/): “a minimum of three additional postnatal checkups.” [HealthHub, undated](https://www.healthhub.sg/well-being-and-lifestyle/pregnancy-and-infant-health/baby-babys-here-whats-next): “You will see your doctor again 4-6 weeks later.”  
    Distinguish WHO’s model from automatic local appointment provision. Follow discharge arrangements; concerns should be assessed earlier. Avoid turning E27’s 21-day rule into general advice to postpone contraception counselling.

19. **Must change | E18–21; C1–2: no universal loss count or trying-again instruction.**  
    [RCOG, 19 June 2023](https://www.rcog.org.uk/guidance/browse-all-guidance/green-top-guidelines/recurrent-miscarriage-green-top-guideline-no-17/): “three or more first trimester miscarriages,” with discretion to investigate after two. ESHRE uses two.  
    Offer support after any loss. Trying again depends on diagnosis, treatment and readiness; KKH distinguishes methotrexate-treated ectopic pregnancy. Keep compassionate reassurance without absolute claims that every possible stress exposure is irrelevant.

20. **Should change | E29–33; D1–4: local age, variable duration and missing early menopause.**  
    [KKH and Annex A, 20 February 2026](https://www.kkh.com.sg/news/announcements/kkh-mchri-launches-singapore-s-first-set-of-menopause-management): “49 years.” Use **around 49 in Singapore**, not a universal 51. Add premature ovarian insufficiency, early and treatment-induced menopause. E31 measures vasomotor symptom duration, not the duration of perimenopause; do not use it to validate a fixed four-to-eight-year course.

21. **Should change | evidence currency: retain explicit verification gaps.**  
    [IMS, 23 December 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41433054/), has an “Erratum in” dated **25 March 2026**; the correction’s contents remain unverified. The Menopause Society still lists its 2022 HT and 2023 nonhormone statements. A [new ACOG menopause guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42878500/) has a November 2026 issue date; its pre-cutoff online publication and full recommendations remain unresolved.  
    [NG194](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng194/) confirms 9 June 2026, but the amendment concerns “vitamin K prophylaxis.” NG23/88/126/257 pages remained inaccessible: their supplied dates and changes are **not independently certified**. This is not a complete retraction clearance for every ledger row.

22. **Should change | missing topics and format: prioritise clinical navigation.**  
    Add contraception/emergency contraception and sexual health; PMS/PMDD; breast changes; pelvic-floor recovery; persistent fatigue assessment including thyroid and iron-related causes; and optional pathways for stillbirth, termination and relationship safety. [ACOG, 1 December 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37973069/) explicitly covers “premenstrual dysphoric disorder.”  
    These are reviewer priorities, not a claim that specialists were consulted. Remove menarche from this adult collection. Split C1–2, C4, D3 and F1 into narrow clinician-reviewed modules with accompanying text. Use voluntary opening and content notices for loss, violence and psychiatric emergencies. Abortion law, medication protocols and individual risk calculations should not be compressed into generic clips.

23. **Must change | A2, E1–3: remove hormone optimisation narratives.**  
    [Schlie, 22 July 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40695607/): “Maximum and explosive strength remained largely unaffected,” but other findings varied. Thus neither universal cycle-training prescriptions nor “at most trivial” across every outcome is justified.  
    [Tucker, 15 July 2024](https://academic.oup.com/nutritionreviews/article/83/3/e866/7713894): “Energy intake was found to be greater in the LP.” That supports acknowledging appetite variation, not special phase diets. “Hormones lift energy and skin” and “strength training makes you thrive” remain unsupported promises. For sleep, [AASM, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7853203/) recommends “multicomponent cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia”; sleep habits alone are not its treatment equivalent.

24. **Should change | all topics: weakest-source deletion result.**  
    My sensitivity assessment of the supplied ledger and opened sources:

    - **Core messages survive:** A1, A3–4, A6, B1–2, C1, C3–4, D2–5, E1, E3 and F1, after the qualifications above.
    - **Source-dependent details:** A5’s rename is one consensus initiative; B3’s timing, C2’s loss count and C5’s operational contraception rules depend on selected guidelines. D1’s local age estimate and E31’s exact durations should remain source-labelled.
    - **Not adequately supported initially:** A2’s energy/skin promises, E2’s full life-stage nutrient prescription, E4’s app-specific benefits and F2’s predictive accuracy.

    Removing weak prevalence studies changes numerical hooks, not help-seeking advice. Repeated press releases are not independent corroboration. [FIGO 2023](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijgo.14946) itself makes interpretation “age dependent.”

25. **Must change | T5, A2: the MP4 service-worker branch is unsafe for partial responses.**  
    Inspection of `sw.js` at `fe04055` found cache-first MP4 handling and `cache.put` without range handling. The [Service Worker specification, draft 17 September 2026](https://w3c.github.io/ServiceWorker/#cache-put), rejects status 206 with a “TypeError.”  
    Recommend normal network/browser HTTP caching initially. Offline video requires deliberate complete-response downloads, quota management and correct byte-range responses. This is a verified code issue, not a reproduced playback failure.

26. **Should change | T1–4: progressive MP4 is a reasonable initial choice; HLS is not required by clip length.**  
    [Apple, undated](https://developer.apple.com/streaming/): HLS “dynamically adapts to network conditions.” Selecting by player size does not provide that adaptation. Start with 720p, optional 1080p for larger playback, and measure startup/stalling on phones. Budget both renditions against [GitHub’s limits, undated](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits): “soft bandwidth limit of 100 GB per month.” Apple’s illustrative HLS ladder is not a mandatory encoding recipe.

27. **Must change | E45, T6: captions alone do not complete accessibility.**  
    [WCAG 2.2, 5 October 2023](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) requires prerecorded captions under 1.2.2. At A, 1.2.3 permits an alternative or audio description; AA’s 1.2.5 requires audio description where meaningful visuals are otherwise unavailable. W3C clarifies: “Where all of the video information is already provided in existing audio, no additional audio description is necessary.”  
    Narrate thresholds and meaningful diagrams, provide checked WebVTT captions and a descriptive transcript, and test controls. Burned captions are optional, not required; selectable captions support adjustment and translation.

28. **Note | E42, F1: separate emergency response from emotional support.**  
    [SCDF, undated](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/home/about-scdf/emergency-medical-services): “Call 995 only for Emergency.” Use **995** for medical emergencies. The required hotline lookup verified [national mindline 1771](tel:1771?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline); SOS details were not independently reverified in this review. Keep care information accessible without playing a video.

**Claims I would reject in every script:**

- “Rhythme pinpoints ovulation” or identifies safe contraceptive days.
- “MHT is safe for everyone” or prevents dementia.
- “Mindfulness treats hot flushes” or improves fertility.
- “Your hormones determine your energy, skin or ideal diet.”
- “Wait for more bleeding, a positive test or two weeks of severe mood symptoms.”
- “Normal screening, a normal scan or an app estimate means nothing is wrong.”
