Warmth, without a prescription

Simple comfort and practitioner-led treatment are different choices.

A wrapped warm pack for comfort

For ordinary period cramps, a wrapped warm pack may feel helpful. Keep it comfortably warm, follow the product instructions and stop if it hurts or feels too hot. Seek care for severe or persistent pain.

Moxa belongs in a care conversation

Moxibustion burns material near the body and involves heat and smoke. This app does not give a home technique, treatment point or pregnancy-safety assurance. Discuss it with a qualified clinician and practitioner.

A topical tradition is not a fertility treatment

This page does not provide a castor-oil pack or herbal-poultice protocol. Traditional use and laboratory findings do not establish a human ovarian benefit or a safe individual regimen.
