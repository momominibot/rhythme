Tradition meets careful questions

What can the research tell us?

Period discomfort

Some trials report less pain with acupressure, including SP6. Evidence remains limited by study quality and inconsistent protocols. It does not establish a personal treatment routine.

Fertility & ovarian health

This atlas does not show that pressing an acupoint improves egg quality, ovarian reserve or pregnancy chances. Being included in a study protocol does not prove that a point works.

Menopause symptoms

Recent IMS guidance finds insufficient evidence for manual acupuncture for hot flashes, while allowing clinician discussion of electroacupuncture. This does not establish a benefit from pressing any point in this atlas. A clinician can help you compare established options.

Sources checked 30 September 2026. Clinical review of this atlas is pending.

When comfort practices can wait

Get urgent medical advice for pelvic pain if pregnancy is possible. Severe or worsening pain, fainting, shoulder-tip pain, difficulty breathing or heavy vaginal bleeding needs emergency assessment.
