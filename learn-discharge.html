Getting to know your discharge

Source-checked 30 September 2026. Personal advice belongs in a conversation with your clinician.

Variation can be ordinary

Discharge may be clear or white, thicker or more slippery, and its amount can change. You do not need to match a perfect colour chart.

Observe without diagnosing

Record the date, texture and any discomfort. A discharge photo cannot tell Ovuluna whether you have an infection, which hormone is high, or whether you ovulated. Cervical-fluid awareness is one signal with practical limitations.

When to ask for help

Seek care for a new unpleasant smell, itching, soreness, painful urination, pelvic pain or bleeding between periods or after sex. The appearance alone cannot identify the cause. Avoid douching or perfumed products inside the vagina.
