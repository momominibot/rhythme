What Your Partner Should Know

A quick guide to each phase — share this to help them understand your rhythm i.
🩸Menstrual
Days 1–5. Energy is low; body is shedding and resetting.
Partner tip: Offer warmth, quiet, and patience. Help with chores so she can rest.
🌱Follicular
Days 6–13. Estrogen rises; energy and confidence build.
Partner tip: Plan active dates, adventures, and creative projects together.
✨Ovulation
Days 14–16. Peak energy, sociability, and libido.
Partner tip: This is a great time for connection, romance, and social plans.
🌙Luteal
Days 17–28. Progesterone rises; PMS possible before next period.
Partner tip: Be extra supportive. Mood swings and cravings are normal i.

Intimacy Guide

Desire and comfort shift across the cycle. Here's how to connect at each stage.
💕
Ovulation window
Libido naturally peaks around ovulation due to rising estrogen i. This is often a time of heightened connection.
🤗
Luteal phase
Some experience tender breasts or lower desire. Non-sexual intimacy — cuddling, massage — can be deeply connecting i.
🗣️
Communication is key
Checking in verbally builds trust. Partners who communicate about cycle needs report higher relationship satisfaction i.

Conversation Starters

Use these prompts to open a dialogue with your partner about your cycle and needs.
"I'm in my luteal phase right now, which means I might feel a bit more sensitive. Can we take it easy this weekend?"
Sets expectations and invites support without blame.
"I've noticed I have more energy around day 10–14. Want to plan a hike or date night then?"
Frames cycle awareness as a positive planning tool.
"When I'm menstruating, what's one small thing you could do that would help me feel supported?"
Invites your partner to contribute and feel involved.
"I've been tracking my sleep and stress. Would you be open to trying a 5-minute breathing exercise with me tonight?"
Turns wellness into a shared, bonding activity i.
