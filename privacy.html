What this build does

Your diary
Periods, symptoms, pain, mood, sleep, meals and food photo thumbnails, weight, calm minutes and your getting-to-know-you answers (which can include pregnancy, hormones, medicines and health history) are saved in this browser on this phone. There is no account and no Rhythme cloud yet, and no app-layer encryption, so anyone who can open this browser profile may be able to read them. Clearing the browser or losing the phone can lose them.
Luna’s AI help (optional, off unless you turn it on)
When it is on, a note the on-phone rules cannot read, a food photo, or a 7 or 28 day summary for a journal reading goes through Rhythme’s server to a third-party AI service in China, and the answer comes back to you. No name is attached, but what you write can still say a lot about your health. Rhythme does not keep what Luna reads. Turn each part on or off in Luna’s settings.
Rhythme Plus codes
When you use an access code, Rhythme’s server records that the code was used, with a scrambled form of your network address, to stop misuse. Plus episodes stream through short-lived links. Nothing from your diary is sent.
Ordinary service records
Our web host keeps standard connection logs, fonts load from Google, and free podcast episodes stream from their podcast host. There are no analytics and no advertising, and we do not sell your data.
Help make Rhythme better
Off unless you tick it. Nothing is shared today. If we add it, we will explain exactly what de-identified patterns would be used and ask before it starts. Research would always be asked about separately.
Before anything changes
Before cloud backup or any new use starts, the app will show a new versioned notice and ask you again. Current notice: version 2, 18 October 2026.

Changing browser, origin or app installation can show a different set of records. These controls do not delete accounts, other devices, previously downloaded files or records held by a service.

Delete or export local data

No items are selected automatically. Legacy entries keep their original contents unless you explicitly choose to delete them. Unrecognized storage keys are left untouched.

You can prepare an export before deleting. Choosing items or opening the review does not delete anything.
