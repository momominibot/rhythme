Understanding ovarian age

A clearer look at ovarian reserve, test results and the limits of a single number.

Reserve is one part of the picture

AMH and antral follicle count (AFC) are used in ovarian reserve assessment. Reserve describes egg quantity, which is distinct from egg quality. These measurements do not, by themselves, establish a biological ovarian age.

What a test can tell you

AMH and AFC can inform expected response to ovarian stimulation. ACOG advises against using one AMH result to predict time to pregnancy in women with presumed fertility.

What Ovuluna has established

A clinically validated Ovuluna ovarian-age formula has not been established in this release. Published background research does not validate this app’s combined score or its age offsets.

Start with your actual records

Keep the result, unit, test date and laboratory reference interval together. Bring the original report when discussing your results with a clinician.

Questions for your appointment
